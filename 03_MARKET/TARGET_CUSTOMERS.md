## Purpose

Define the customer segments the business intends to understand and serve.
This document supports research, service design, content planning, and CRM
segmentation. It does not confirm geographic coverage, commercial terms, or
service availability.

## Primary Segments

### Residential

- Apartment owners and tenants
- Villa and townhouse residents
- Residential building stakeholders
- Families seeking cleaning, maintenance, or pest-control support

Common needs include convenient booking, clear communication, trusted service
personnel, predictable scheduling, and visible service quality.

### Commercial

- Offices
- Retail businesses
- Restaurants and cafés
- Clinics and salons

Common needs include scheduled work, minimal disruption, documented service
delivery, and a dependable point of contact.

### Property and Facility Management

- Residential property managers
- Commercial property managers
- Building and facility operators

Common needs include coordinated requests, preventive maintenance, incident
response, service records, and consistent quality across properties.

### Hospitality

- Hotels
- Hotel apartments
- Holiday-home operators

Common needs include high cleanliness standards, flexible scheduling,
professional presentation, and rapid operational coordination.

### Corporate and Government

Organizations that may require formal approvals, documented quality controls,
defined service scopes, and accountable service delivery.

## Decision Factors to Validate

The existing research identifies the following likely decision factors. They
remain hypotheses until confirmed through interviews, sales data, reviews, or
analytics:

- Trust and reputation
- Response and scheduling speed
- Clear scope and pricing communication
- Professional staff and presentation
- Service quality and follow-up
- Reviews and referrals

## Usage Rules

- Do not infer confirmed service areas from a customer segment.
- Do not publish pricing, response-time, warranty, or compliance claims from
  this document.
- Website, CRM, campaigns, and AI systems should reference stable segment IDs
  once the CRM data model is approved.
- Geographic targeting must come only from `SERVICE_AREAS.md` after owner
  approval.

# Industry Overview

The UAE maintenance and cleaning industry is one of the fastest-growing service sectors due to:

- Rapid urban development.
- Increasing residential communities.
- Commercial expansion.
- Growing demand for professional facility services.
- Higher customer expectations for quality and speed.

# Market Trends

## Purpose

This document identifies the major trends shaping the maintenance, cleaning, and pest control industry in the UAE and explains how AFAQ Alhayat can benefit from them.

---

# Digital Transformation

Customers increasingly expect:

- Online booking
- Fast response
- Mobile-friendly websites
- Digital communication
- Instant quotations

---

# Local SEO Growth

More customers search using:

- Google Search
- Google Maps
- "Near Me" searches
- Location-based keywords

Local SEO has become a critical growth factor.

Businesses are adopting:

- WhatsApp Business
- AI Chat Assistants
- Automated Booking
- Email Automation
- SMS Notifications

# Customer Expectations

Modern customers expect:

- Same-day responses

- Reduce waste

- Protect the environment
- Follow health and safety standards

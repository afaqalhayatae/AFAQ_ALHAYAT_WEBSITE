# Market

## Purpose

Owns market intelligence, customer segments, geographic coverage, and
commercial prioritization without duplicating brand, service, or channel facts.

## Core Authorities

- `SERVICE_AREAS.md` — canonical geographic coverage and stable location IDs.
- `HIGH_VALUE_AREA_PRIORITY.md` — owner-authorized premium-area marketing priorities.
- `TARGET_CUSTOMERS.md` — target customer framework.
- `COMPETITOR_ANALYSIS.md` — competitor intelligence.

Coverage and marketing priority are different: every approved area may be
served, while investment priority changes according to evidence and capacity.

# Luxury Design Direction

**Project:** AFAQ Alhayat Enterprise Platform  
**Document Type:** Binding Design Standard  
**Status:** Approved Direction  
**Applies To:** Website, landing pages, customer portal, dashboards, campaigns, and future digital products

---

## 1. Design Ambition

AFAQ Alhayat must present itself as a premium UAE service company with the clarity, confidence, and consistency expected from an international brand.

Luxury in this project means:

- Calm confidence rather than visual noise.
- Precision rather than decoration.
- Generous space rather than crowded layouts.
- Authentic evidence rather than exaggerated claims.
- Excellent Arabic and English experiences rather than translated copies.
- Fast, accessible interfaces rather than effects that slow the user down.

The experience must feel refined, trustworthy, modern, and easy to use on every screen size.

---

## 2. Brand Experience Principles

### Quiet Luxury

Use a restrained visual language. Every color, image, line, icon, and animation must have a clear purpose.

### Operational Trust

The design must make service scope, process, safety guidance, availability, and next steps easy to understand.

### Human Professionalism

Show trained people, clean equipment, careful work, and respectful customer interactions. The brand must feel capable and approachable.

### Bilingual Equality

Arabic RTL and English LTR are equal products. Neither version may feel secondary, compressed, or mechanically translated.

### Evidence Before Claims

Use verified reviews, real project photography, approved credentials, measurable service information, and clear policies. Never use unsupported badges, awards, guarantees, statistics, or certifications.

---

## 3. Visual Language

### Color

The approved tokens in `COLORS.md` remain the source of truth.

- Use Primary Blue as the main brand and action color.
- Use white and soft neutral surfaces to create space and clarity.
- Use dark text for editorial contrast and authority.
- Reserve green, amber, and red for genuine system states.
- Use WhatsApp green only for the official WhatsApp action.
- Do not introduce decorative gold gradients, neon colors, or unapproved accent palettes.
- Do not use multiple strong colors in the same section.
- All text and interactive states must meet the approved accessibility contrast standard.

### Typography

The approved families in `TYPOGRAPHY.md` remain the source of truth.

- Use a confident editorial hierarchy with few, meaningful sizes.
- Keep headings concise and give them generous surrounding space.
- Maintain comfortable Arabic line height and balanced English measure.
- Avoid excessive bold text, all-caps paragraphs, condensed type, and decorative fonts.
- Numbers, prices, dates, and measurements must remain easy to scan in both languages.

### Space and Grid

- Use generous whitespace as a primary design element.
- Build pages on a consistent responsive grid.
- Keep readable content width controlled; do not stretch paragraphs across wide screens.
- Prefer fewer, stronger sections over many small cards.
- Align content, images, and actions precisely across related pages.
- Mobile spacing must feel intentional, not like a compressed desktop page.

### Surfaces and Depth

- Prefer clean surfaces, subtle borders, and restrained elevation.
- Use shadows only when they explain hierarchy or interaction.
- Avoid glass effects, heavy gradients, glowing borders, and excessive rounded containers.
- Repeated cards must have consistent padding, radius, border, and hover behavior.

### Iconography

- Use one coherent icon family.
- Prefer simple line icons with consistent stroke weight.
- Icons support meaning; they do not replace clear labels.
- Do not mix emoji, filled icons, outline icons, and illustrations within one interface.

## 4. Photography and Media Direction

Approved imagery should feel real, premium, and specific to professional service delivery in the UAE.

Prioritize:

- Real technicians following approved working and safety procedures.
- Clean uniforms, organized equipment, and well-maintained vehicles.
- High-quality residential and commercial environments.
- Natural or carefully controlled lighting.
- Wide environmental frames, detailed work shots, and respectful human moments.
- Consistent color grading across the website.

Reject:

- Generic stock images that do not represent the company or service.
- Visibly artificial people, tools, uniforms, locations, or results.
- Fear-based pest imagery used excessively.
- Misleading before-and-after comparisons.
- Images exposing private customer information or locations without permission.
- Low-resolution, oversaturated, distorted, or watermarked assets.

Every image must have a business purpose, descriptive filename, correct dimensions, optimized delivery format, and meaningful alternative text where required.

## 5. Core Interface Direction

### Header and Navigation

- Use a clean, stable header with clear Arabic and English navigation.
- Keep the primary booking action visible without overpowering the brand.
- Phone and WhatsApp actions must use canonical verified contact data only.
- On mobile, navigation and contact actions must be reachable without clutter.

### Hero Sections

- Communicate one clear promise, one supporting statement, and one primary action.
- Use authentic service or brand imagery with controlled contrast.
- Avoid rotating sliders, multiple competing headlines, and excessive badges.

### Buttons and Calls to Action

- Each section should have one dominant action.
- Use direct labels such as Book a Service, Request a Quote, Call, or WhatsApp.
- Never use vague labels such as Click Here.
- Primary, secondary, and text actions must have distinct and consistent styles.
- Hover, focus, loading, disabled, success, and error states are mandatory.

### Service Cards

- Show the service name, concise benefit, relevant image or icon, and clear destination.
- Keep card heights and content density consistent.
- Do not place unverifiable prices, response times, guarantees, or credentials on cards.

### Forms and Booking

- Ask only for information needed at the current step.
- Use clear labels, helpful validation, and visible privacy context.
- Provide progress feedback for multi-step booking.
- Confirm successful submission and explain the next step.
- Never rely on placeholders as the only field labels.

### Trust Sections

Trust must be earned through approved evidence:

- Verified customer reviews.
- Real service process.
- Approved credentials and compliance information.
- Clear safety and customer guidance.
- Real project media.
- Transparent contact and service-area information.

Unverified items must remain hidden from public interfaces.

## 6. Page-Level Experience

### Homepage

The homepage must establish the brand, explain core service categories, present verified trust evidence, show geographic relevance, and guide visitors toward booking or enquiry without trying to contain every detail.

### Service Pages

Each service page must use canonical service knowledge and include:

- Clear customer problem and service outcome.
- Included and excluded scope where approved.
- Service process.
- Safety or preparation guidance where relevant.
- Frequently asked questions.
- Related services.
- Verified local availability.
- One primary conversion action.

### Location Pages

Location pages must provide genuine local value. They must not be mass-produced copies with only the place name changed. Each publishable page requires approved service coverage, unique local context, canonical contact data, useful internal links, and content quality review.

### Landing Pages

Landing pages may support Google Ads and campaigns, but they must also work as high-quality standalone experiences.

- Match the visitor's intent and campaign promise.
- Keep navigation focused without hiding essential trust or legal information.
- Use one primary conversion goal.
- Preserve canonical brand, contact, service, and location facts.
- Measure conversions without sacrificing privacy, speed, or accessibility.
- Never make paid traffic a substitute for useful organic content.

### Booking and Contact Experience

The booking journey must be short, reassuring, mobile-first, and explicit about what happens next. Phone and WhatsApp actions must use only verified canonical data.

---

## 7. Motion and Interaction

- Motion must explain state, hierarchy, or continuity.
- Typical interface transitions should remain subtle and brief.
- Avoid continuous movement, parallax overload, animated backgrounds, and attention-seeking effects.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Interaction must remain fully usable by keyboard and assistive technology.
- No animation may delay access to essential content or actions.

---

## 8. Responsive and Bilingual Quality

- Design mobile-first, then expand deliberately for tablet and desktop.
- Test common small-screen widths and long Arabic labels.
- Mirroring RTL layout must preserve meaning, order, and familiar controls.
- Phone numbers, URLs, email addresses, and mixed-direction content require deliberate bidirectional handling.
- Images must retain their focal point in both language layouts.
- Arabic and English pages must use equivalent information architecture, functionality, and quality.

---

## 9. Performance and Accessibility

Premium quality includes speed and inclusion.

- Follow WCAG 2.2 AA wherever applicable.
- Maintain visible keyboard focus and logical focus order.
- Use semantic headings and landmarks.
- Provide adequate touch targets and error recovery.
- Optimize images and avoid unnecessary scripts, fonts, and effects.
- Prevent layout shifts and protect core reading and booking journeys.
- Performance must be validated on mobile networks, not desktop preview alone.

---

## 10. Prohibited Patterns

Do not use:

- Cheap gold-on-black luxury clichés.
- Excessive gradients, shadows, glass effects, or animation.
- Auto-playing audio or intrusive video.
- Carousels for critical information.
- Fake urgency, countdown timers, or misleading scarcity.
- Unsupported awards, ratings, certifications, guarantees, or statistics.
- Placeholder phone numbers or unverified service areas.
- Keyword-stuffed headings and repetitive location copy.
- Pop-ups that obstruct essential content on arrival.
- Inconsistent components created for one page only.
- AI-generated media presented as real company evidence.

---

## 11. Design Acceptance Gate

A design is not ready for implementation or publication until all applicable checks pass:

- [ ] The page has one clear purpose and primary action.
- [ ] Visual treatment follows approved color, typography, spacing, and component rules.
- [ ] The layout feels calm, spacious, and precise on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Arabic RTL and English LTR have been reviewed independently.
- [ ] Contact, service, location, and trust data come from canonical approved sources.
- [ ] No placeholder or unverified fact is publishable.
- [ ] Images are authentic, approved, optimized, and correctly described.
- [ ] Heading hierarchy, navigation, keyboard use, contrast, and forms are accessible.
- [ ] Motion is restrained and reduced-motion behavior exists.
- [ ] The page meets performance budgets and has no avoidable layout shift.
- [ ] Internal links follow the approved URL and linking standard.
- [ ] SEO and structured data reflect visible, verified content.
- [ ] Analytics events measure useful outcomes without duplicating or exposing sensitive data.
- [ ] Quality review has approved the final bilingual experience.

---

## 12. Governance

This document defines the visual direction; it does not replace the detailed token and component standards in this folder.

When rules conflict:

1. Verified business and safety information takes precedence.
2. Accessibility and usability take precedence over decoration.
3. Existing approved design tokens take precedence over page-level invention.
4. Canonical brand, contact, market, and service documents take precedence over copied content.
5. Any exception must be documented and approved before implementation.

The target is not to imitate another company. The target is to achieve the same level of discipline, confidence, consistency, and execution quality expected from leading international brands while preserving AFAQ Alhayat's own identity.

# Content Strategy

## Objective

Turn approved company knowledge into useful bilingual content that supports
customer decisions, service delivery, search discovery, and long-term trust.

## Content Pillars

- Service education and scope
- Preparation, safety, and aftercare
- Maintenance and prevention guidance
- Customer decision support
- Business trust and process transparency
- Approved location-specific information

## Content Lifecycle

1. Brief from a verified customer need or search intent.
2. Retrieve canonical facts from the owning domain.
3. Draft in the primary language.
4. Produce an equivalent localized version, not a literal translation.
5. Fact, safety, brand, legal, and SEO review.
6. Owner approval where commercial claims are involved.
7. Publish with version and source references.
8. Measure performance and schedule review.
9. Update, consolidate, redirect, or retire.

## Prohibited Content

- Invented prices, discounts, guarantees, licenses, reviews, or statistics
- Unapproved location and availability claims
- Unsafe technical instructions
- Near-duplicate pages created only to target keywords
- AI-generated media presented as real work without disclosure

## Required Metadata

Every content item needs content ID, owner, audience, intent, service IDs,
location IDs where applicable, language, sources, status, approver, publish
date, review date, channel, and performance owner.

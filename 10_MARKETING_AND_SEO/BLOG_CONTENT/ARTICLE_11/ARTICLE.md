## Key points (EN)
- Carpets and upholstery trap dust and allergens differently than hard floors, requiring a different cleaning approach.
- The general link to allergies/respiratory comfort with long-term buildup (general framing, not a medical claim).
- Service steps: assessing fabric/carpet type, targeted cleaning, stain treatment, proper drying.
- Suggested frequency: every few months depending on use.

## Main Content (English)

Moving is one of the most common reasons customers book deep cleaning. Here's what's useful to know.

### Why Deep Cleaning Suits a Move

- Reaches buildup-prone areas routine cleaning skips.
- Well suited to move-in/move-out or occasional deep resets.
- A more thorough result than a standard cleaning visit.
- Scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- Clear scope agreed with you before the visit.

### What Affects Cost

Pricing depends on property size and condition — this is subject to company policy, and our team can provide an accurate quote once they understand your property.

### Before You Book

Let us know whether the property is occupied or empty, and roughly how long it's been since the last deep clean — this helps our team plan the visit and scope.

## FAQ

**How often is deep cleaning recommended?**
Many customers book deep cleaning every few months, or before/after a major event such as moving.

**Is deep cleaning covered by a satisfaction guarantee?**
Any satisfaction terms are confirmed directly with our team at the time of booking — please confirm this before the visit.

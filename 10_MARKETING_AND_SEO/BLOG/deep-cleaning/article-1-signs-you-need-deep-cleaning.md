## Main Content (English)

Some cleaning tasks build up gradually and need a more intensive session to address. Here are six signs it may be time for deep cleaning.

**1. Grout and tile lines with visible buildup.** These areas aren't part of routine cleaning and accumulate over time.

**2. Grease and grime behind or under kitchen appliances.** Easy to overlook day-to-day, but noticeable once addressed.

**3. Dust buildup on skirting boards, vents, and light fixtures.** Areas that routine cleaning typically doesn't reach.

**4. A property that hasn't been deep-cleaned in a long time.** If it's been months, a reset can make a real difference.

**5. Preparing a home for a special occasion or after an extended absence.** A useful step before hosting or returning to a property.

**6. Move-in or move-out cleaning needs.** One of the most common reasons customers book deep cleaning.

Our deep cleaning service covers grout and tile scrubbing, behind and under kitchen appliance cleaning where accessible, skirting board and vent dusting, detailed bathroom descaling, and all standard general cleaning tasks in the same visit.

## FAQ

**How often is deep cleaning recommended?**
Many customers book deep cleaning every few months, or before/after a major event such as moving. A technician can suggest a suitable frequency based on your property.

**How much does deep cleaning cost?**
Pricing depends on property size and condition. This is subject to company policy — please contact us directly for an accurate quote.

## Main Content (English)

Small jobs have a way of piling up on a to-do list. Here are six everyday tasks a handyman visit can cover.

**1. A door or cabinet that doesn't close properly.** A common, quick-to-diagnose issue.

**2. Loose hinges, handles, or fittings.** These are exactly the kind of small fix that's easy to put off.

**3. Furniture assembly or minor furniture repair.** A useful option if flat-pack assembly isn't how you want to spend your weekend.

**4. Small wall-mounting jobs.** Shelves, mirrors, and curtain rods all fall into this category.

**5. Minor carpentry touch-ups.** Small fixes that don't require a dedicated carpentry project.

**6. General small repairs that don't fit a single trade category.** If it's not clearly plumbing, electrical, or AC work, it's likely handyman territory.

Our handyman service covers door, cabinet, and hinge adjustment, furniture assembly and minor repair, wall-mounting, minor carpentry and fitting repairs, general small-item installation, and multi-task visits combining several small jobs in one call-out.

## FAQ

**Can a handyman visit cover several small jobs at once?**
Yes — many customers group several small tasks into one visit. Let us know the list of jobs when booking so we can plan the visit accordingly.

**How do I know if my task needs a handyman or a specialist trade?**
As a general guide, small repairs, assembly, and mounting jobs suit a handyman visit, while major electrical, plumbing, or AC work suits our dedicated trade services. If you're unsure, describe the task when contacting us and we'll advise.

## Main Content (English)

If you're not sure which cleaning service fits your needs, here's how to decide — and what to expect once you book.

### General Cleaning vs. Deep Cleaning

General cleaning covers routine upkeep — dusting, floors, kitchen/bathroom surfaces — while deep cleaning is a more intensive session covering areas not part of routine cleaning, such as grout or behind appliances. Many customers use general cleaning for regular upkeep and deep cleaning occasionally.

### Why Recurring General Cleaning Is Convenient

- Keeps a property consistently tidy between deeper cleans.
- Flexible for one-time or recurring scheduling.
- Frees up time for busy households and offices.
- Scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- A consistent cleaning checklist followed on every visit.

### What Affects Cost

Pricing depends on property size and cleaning frequency — this is subject to company policy, and our team can provide an accurate quote once they understand your property.

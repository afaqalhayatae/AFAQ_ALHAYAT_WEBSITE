## Main Content (English)

Cockroaches are one of the most common pests reported in UAE homes, especially in kitchens and areas with food and moisture. Common signs of activity include sightings (especially at night), a persistent musty odor, and small dark droppings near cabinets or appliances.

### Why Professional Treatment Matters

Cockroach infestations often start small and go unnoticed until they've spread. Our pest control service begins with a documented inspection and treatment process for every visit — never guesswork — assessing your property's specific situation before recommending a plan.

### What to Expect

A technician assesses the affected areas, explains the recommended approach, and carries out treatment using an approved product and method suited to your property. Duration depends on the property size and the type of infestation, and the technician provides an estimated time before starting.

## Main Content (English)

Termites can cause damage to timber and structures gradually, often before it's clearly visible. Common signs include damaged or hollow-sounding timber, small mud tubes along walls or foundations, and discarded wings near windows or doors.

### Why Inspection Matters

Because termite damage builds up out of sight, our pest control service begins with a documented, on-site inspection of affected timber and structures — assessing the extent of activity before recommending a property-specific treatment plan.

### What to Expect

A technician inspects the affected areas, explains the recommended approach, and carries out treatment using an approved product and method suited to your property and the extent of activity found.

## FAQ

**What pests does AFAQ AL HAYAT treat?**
Cockroaches, ants, bed bugs, rodents, termites, mosquitoes, flies, fleas, and other common pests.

**How long does pest control treatment take?**
Duration depends on the property size and the type of infestation. The technician provides an estimated time before starting.

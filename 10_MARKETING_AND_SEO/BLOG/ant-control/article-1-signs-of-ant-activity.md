## Main Content (English)

Ants are among the most common household pests in the UAE, often noticed as a visible trail leading to a food or water source. Common signs include ant trails along walls or counters, small entry points near windows or doors, and activity concentrated around kitchens or outdoor areas.

### Why Professional Treatment Matters

Ant colonies can be more extensive than what's visible on the surface. Our pest control service begins with a documented inspection — assessing where the trail leads and the scale of activity — before recommending a property-specific treatment plan.

### What to Expect

A technician inspects the affected trail and surrounding areas, explains the recommended approach, and carries out treatment using an approved product and method suited to your property and the ant activity observed.

## Main Content (English)

Choosing a color and booking a painter don't have to be separate headaches — here's how the two come together.

### Getting Color Guidance

Our team can offer general color guidance based on your space and preference during the on-site consultation — a useful step if you're unsure what will work best in a given room's lighting and layout.

### Why It's Worth Booking

- Refreshed, consistent appearance across painted surfaces.
- Help covering stains, cracks, or patch marks evenly.
- Scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- Clear explanation of the plan before any work starts.

### What Affects Cost

Pricing depends on the area size, surface condition, and paint chosen — this is subject to company policy, and our team can provide an accurate quote for your property once they understand the scope.

## FAQ

**Can you help me choose a paint color?**
Yes, our team can offer general color guidance based on your space and preference during the on-site consultation.

**How much does painting a room cost?**
Pricing depends on the area size, surface condition, and paint chosen. This is subject to company policy — please contact us directly for an accurate quote for your property.

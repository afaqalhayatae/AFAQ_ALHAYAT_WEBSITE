# Paid Media Strategy

## Document Information

- **Owner:** Marketing
- **Status:** Draft framework — budget, accounts, targets, and offers require approval
- **Version:** 1.0

## Strategic Position

Google Ads and other paid channels are accelerators, not the foundation of the
business. The company must retain useful service pages, strong landing
experiences, organic discoverability, direct demand, referrals, and owned
customer relationships whether advertising is active or paused.

## Permitted Uses

- Test approved service and audience demand.
- Accelerate a validated landing page or seasonal need.
- Enter a new approved market with controlled measurement.
- Recover qualified visitors through policy-compliant remarketing.
- Learn which messages and intents produce qualified leads.

Paid traffic must not compensate for weak content, unverified offers, poor
service definition, broken conversion paths, or missing measurement.

## Launch Preconditions

- Approved service package and location coverage
- Verified contact/booking paths
- Dedicated landing page that matches the search intent and advertisement
- Clear, accurate offer with no invented price, guarantee, license, urgency, or
  response-time claim
- Privacy, consent, call, form, and offline-lead measurement reviewed
- Negative-keyword, geographic, schedule, device, and budget controls
- Named owner for lead response and outcome feedback

## Measurement Hierarchy

### Primary business outcomes

- Qualified lead
- Confirmed booking
- Completed service
- Approved revenue or margin outcome when the finance model exists

### Secondary diagnostic actions

- Form submission
- Verified phone call
- WhatsApp initiation
- Booking start
- Booking completion

Clicks, impressions, CTR, and platform-reported leads are diagnostic metrics,
not proof of business value.

## Optimization Rules

- Optimize bidding only toward verified high-value conversion actions.
- Separate campaigns by intent and operationally meaningful service groups.
- Do not mix unapproved areas or unavailable services to increase reach.
- Feed qualified/offline outcomes back only through a privacy-reviewed process.
- Compare paid, organic, direct, referral, and assisted paths before assigning
  credit.
- Pause campaigns when lead handling, availability, measurement, or landing
  quality fails.

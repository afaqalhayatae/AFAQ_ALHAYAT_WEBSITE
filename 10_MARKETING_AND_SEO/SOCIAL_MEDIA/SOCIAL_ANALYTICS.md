# Social Analytics

## Measurement

Measure reach and completion in context, then connect engagement to qualified
inquiries, bookings, customer outcomes, and content-learning objectives where
consent and data quality permit.

## Core Dimensions

Channel, account, content ID, campaign ID, service ID, location ID, language,
format, organic/paid, publish time, audience, and creative variant.

## Quality Rules

- Preserve native platform post IDs and source exports.
- Separate organic, paid, boosted, test, and employee activity.
- Do not combine incompatible platform definitions without labeling.
- Record attribution window and uncertainty.
- Never send direct personal contact details to analytics widgets or reports.

Numeric targets require verified baselines and owner approval.

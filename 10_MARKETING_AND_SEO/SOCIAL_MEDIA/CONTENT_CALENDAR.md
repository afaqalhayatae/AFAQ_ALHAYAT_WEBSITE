# Social Content Calendar

## Purpose

Define the scheduling data model without inventing campaigns or dates.

## Required Fields

- Content ID and campaign ID
- Channel and account
- Objective and audience
- Service and location IDs where approved
- Content pillar and format
- Arabic and English variants
- Asset references and rights status
- Draft owner, reviewers, approver
- Planned publish time and timezone
- Final status and platform post ID
- Measurement window and result link

## Status Flow

Idea → Briefed → Drafted → In Review → Changes Required → Approved →
Scheduled → Published → Measured → Updated/Archived.

Nothing may move to Scheduled without approved facts, assets, accessibility,
rights, and account authorization.

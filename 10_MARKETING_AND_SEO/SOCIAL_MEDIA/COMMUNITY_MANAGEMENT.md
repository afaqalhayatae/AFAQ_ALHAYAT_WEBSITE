# Community Management

## Response Principles

Be helpful, concise, respectful, privacy-aware, and honest about uncertainty.
Never request or expose sensitive personal information in public comments.

## Escalate Immediately

- Safety or health incidents
- Threats, abuse, or suspected fraud
- Complaints involving property damage or payment
- Legal or regulatory questions
- Requests containing personal data
- Media inquiries or high-reach reputational risk

## AI Boundaries

AI may classify, summarize, suggest an approved response, and route the case.
It must not resolve complaints, confirm bookings, promise compensation, quote
unapproved prices, or answer emergencies without human oversight.

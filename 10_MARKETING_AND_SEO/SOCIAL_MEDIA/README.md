# Social Media System

## Purpose

Govern bilingual planning, creation, approval, publishing, engagement, and
measurement across social channels.

## Operating Model

1. Strategy defines audience, objective, channel, and content pillar.
2. Knowledge retrieval supplies approved facts.
3. Production creates copy, image, and video variants.
4. Review checks facts, brand, safety, rights, language, and platform fit.
5. An authorized approver releases the item.
6. Scheduler publishes through approved accounts.
7. Monitoring captures performance and escalates customer or safety issues.

## Non-Negotiable Controls

- Human approval before public posting until an owner explicitly approves a
  lower-risk automation class.
- No fabricated job photos, testimonials, results, prices, scarcity, or
  credentials.
- No customer image, name, property, conversation, or location without consent.
- Generated media must follow the approved disclosure and rights policy.
- Comments involving complaints, emergencies, safety, payment, or personal
  data escalate to a human.
- Credentials and tokens never belong in Markdown or source control.

# TikTok and Snapchat Priority Strategy

**Status:** Draft Execution Strategy / Owner Inputs and Accounts Required

---

## Strategic Role

TikTok and Snapchat are priority reach, trust, and lead-generation channels for short-form visual storytelling in the UAE. They must show the people, process, care, and usefulness behind AFAQ Alhayat—not merely repeat promotional posters.

---

## Content Pillars

1. **Useful education** — prevention, preparation, maintenance, and service guidance from approved knowledge.
2. **Process transparency** — how inspection, preparation, quality control, and follow-up work.
3. **People and standards** — real team, training, equipment, and professionalism with approval.
4. **Problem-to-solution stories** — truthful cases with customer and property consent.
5. **Local relevance** — climate, season, property type, and approved service areas.
6. **Questions answered** — natural replies to frequent customer questions.
7. **Brand trust** — behind the scenes, customer care, and verified company identity.

Prices, guarantees, certifications, results, and geographic coverage require canonical approval.

---

## TikTok Format

- Lead with the customer problem or useful outcome in the opening seconds.
- Prefer real vertical video, clear speech, on-screen text, and human presence.
- Produce Arabic-first variants where audience data supports it, with English variants or subtitles.
- Use native pacing and platform-appropriate edits without sacrificing brand quality.
- Create reply videos from approved recurring questions.
- Use search-friendly spoken phrases, captions, and titles naturally.
- Keep calls to action relevant: save, ask, visit the service page, or request help.

---

## Snapchat Format

- Use concise vertical Stories with clear beginning, process, and next step.
- Show authentic daily operations and useful local guidance.
- Build recurring series rather than disconnected promotional snaps.
- Use Public Profile, Stories, Spotlight, and advertising formats according to account eligibility and content purpose.
- Design for sound-on and sound-off viewing.
- Keep overlays inside safe areas and readable on mobile.

---

## Production System

For every shoot, capture:

- One primary 20–45 second story.
- Multiple short hooks.
- Process close-ups.
- Technician explanation.
- Before/after only when truthful, comparable, and consented.
- Arabic and English voice or subtitle coverage.
- Clean thumbnail frame.
- Reusable still images.

No customer, child, property detail, vehicle plate, document, or private location is published without appropriate consent and privacy review.

---

## Publishing Cadence

Cadence is established after a two-week production-capacity pilot. Quality and consistency take precedence over an arbitrary daily volume.

Start with:

- A sustainable number of high-quality short videos.
- Regular Stories where real operations provide value.
- One weekly analytics review.
- Monthly pillar and format review.

Do not promise a fixed frequency until the team, footage pipeline, approval time, and performance baseline are known.

---

## Lead Handling

- Route comments and messages into a monitored queue.
- Use approved automated welcome messages and suggested questions where supported.
- Do not expose placeholder contact details.
- Qualify service type and location without promising coverage.
- Escalate complaints, safety, urgent, price, and booking matters to a human or approved system.
- Preserve consent and source attribution when creating CRM leads.

---

## Success Measures

- Qualified conversations.
- Service-page visits.
- Booking starts and completed enquiries.
- Saves, shares, and meaningful watch time.
- Profile and link actions.
- Performance by content pillar, service, language, and format.
- Response time and human-handoff quality.
- Content production cost and reuse efficiency.

Follower count and raw views are context metrics, not the final business outcome.

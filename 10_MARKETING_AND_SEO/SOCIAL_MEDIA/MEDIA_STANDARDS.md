# Social Media Standards

## Image Standards

- Use approved logo, colors, type, and safe-area rules.
- Export channel-appropriate aspect ratios from a controlled master.
- Preserve legibility on mobile and provide meaningful alternative text.
- Record source, creator, license, consent, generation method, and expiry.
- Do not represent stock or generated scenes as completed customer work.

## Video Standards

- Define hook, purpose, shot list, narration, captions, CTA, and rights before
  production.
- Include accurate subtitles and review Arabic/English meaning.
- Avoid unsafe demonstrations and unverified before/after claims.
- Retain editable master, clean export, transcript, thumbnail, and metadata.

## Privacy

Remove or obtain consent for faces, vehicle plates, documents, addresses,
screens, customer conversations, and property identifiers.

# Channel Strategy

## Status

Draft — official accounts, priorities, budgets, and audience evidence require
owner verification.

## Channel Selection

Use a channel only when it has a defined audience, content role, operating
owner, response capacity, publishing cadence, and measurable business outcome.
Do not open or automate accounts merely to duplicate every post.

## Planning Matrix

For each approved channel record: account URL, owner, audience, objectives,
formats, language mix, cadence, response SLA, escalation route, conversion
path, paid-media role, and success metrics.

## Content Adaptation

Reuse the underlying idea and approved facts, but adapt hook, length, format,
caption, accessibility, thumbnail, and call to action to the channel.

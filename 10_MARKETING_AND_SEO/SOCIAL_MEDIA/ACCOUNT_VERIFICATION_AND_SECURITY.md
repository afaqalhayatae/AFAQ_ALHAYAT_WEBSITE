# Social Account Verification and Security Standard

**Status:** Approved Standard / Owner Documents Required

---

## Objective

Establish one authentic, secure, organization-controlled presence on each approved social platform and complete every available business-verification process without confusing business verification with a public celebrity or creator badge.

---

## Canonical Account Register

Maintain a restricted register containing:

- Platform.
- Official display name and handle.
- Profile URL and platform account ID.
- Account type and business manager.
- Legal owner.
- Primary and backup administrators.
- Recovery email and phone owner.
- Two-factor authentication status.
- Business verification status.
- Public badge status, if applicable.
- Ad account and pixel/dataset identifiers.
- API or scheduler connection status.
- Last access review.

Secrets and identity documents must never be stored in this repository or a public Notion page.

---

## Identity Consistency

Across all profiles use only approved:

- Company legal and public name.
- Logo and profile imagery.
- Brand description and category.
- Verified website domain.
- Canonical contact and location data.
- Arabic and English biography.
- Link destination.

Do not create multiple competing profiles for the same audience without a documented channel architecture.

---

## Verification Pack

Prepare securely:

- Valid company trade-license or accepted business document.
- Exact legal company name and registration number.
- Verified website and company email domain.
- Legal representative information when required.
- Approved company address and contact information.
- Authorized representative letter if requested.
- Consistent public profiles and brand assets.

Only account owners or authorized administrators submit these documents through the official platform interface.

---

## TikTok

- Use TikTok Business Center owned by the company.
- Link the official TikTok account for account management.
- Complete Business Center and Business Account verification using matching legal records.
- Business verification unlocks business capabilities but is distinct from TikTok’s public blue-check verification.
- Use official automatic-message settings or Content Posting API only after access, consent, and platform review requirements are met.
- An unaudited Direct Post API client may have visibility restrictions; public automation must wait for the applicable platform audit.

Official references:

- https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-business-verification
- https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-tiktok-account-entitlements
- https://developers.tiktok.com/products/content-posting-api

---

## Snapchat

- Create and maintain an official Public Profile and business advertising setup owned by the company.
- Use Snapchat Business Manager and approved partner/API paths for advertising and measurement.
- Do not promise a public verification badge. Snap Star is primarily a creator/public-figure program with authenticity, audience, public presence, content-quality, and safety review.
- Snapchat+ badges are subscription labels and are not identity verification.
- Focus first on authentic profile ownership, public content quality, account security, and consistent brand presence.

Official references:

- https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-gb/articles/45368619831956-About-Account-Labels-on-Snapchat
- https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/partners/apply

---

## Security Baseline

- Hardware security keys or authenticator-based 2FA where supported.
- Unique passwords held in an approved password manager.
- No shared passwords in chat or email.
- Least-privilege roles.
- Backup administrator and documented recovery.
- Quarterly access review.
- Login and publishing alerts.
- Verified recovery domain.
- Immediate incident procedure for impersonation or compromise.

---

## Readiness Gate Per Platform

- [ ] Legal owner and business manager confirmed.
- [ ] Official handle, profile URL, and account ID recorded.
- [ ] Two administrators and recovery path confirmed.
- [ ] 2FA enabled.
- [ ] Bio, logo, website, and contact facts approved.
- [ ] Business verification submitted or status documented.
- [ ] Public badge expectations accurately documented.
- [ ] Content rights and consent process active.
- [ ] Scheduler/API permissions use least privilege.
- [ ] Test post and removal procedure verified.
- [ ] Analytics ownership confirmed.

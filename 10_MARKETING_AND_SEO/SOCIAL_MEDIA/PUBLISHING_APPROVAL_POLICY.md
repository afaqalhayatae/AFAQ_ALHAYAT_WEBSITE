# Publishing Approval Policy

## Approval Classes

- **Low risk:** evergreen educational content using approved facts.
- **Medium risk:** campaigns, offers, location claims, customer stories, or
  generated realistic media.
- **High risk:** prices, guarantees, licenses, safety instructions, legal
  claims, crisis responses, payments, or personal data.

Low-risk content requires the configured content approver. Medium risk also
requires the responsible business owner. High risk requires specialist and
owner approval for every item.

## Release Gate

Verify canonical sources, language parity, assets and rights, CTA and links,
tracking, timing, account, approver, rollback method, and comment-monitoring
owner. Approval is bound to the exact content and asset version.

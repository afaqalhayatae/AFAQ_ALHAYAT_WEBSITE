# AFAQ AL HAYAT — Backlink Strategy

## Document Information

- **Owner:** Business Owner / Marketing
- **Status:** Draft — strategy only; no outreach has been sent, no link has been placed
- **Prepared:** 2026-08-05
- **Scope:** Link-building strategy for the 30-article content plan (`BLOG_CONTENT/CONTENT_PLAN.md`) and the site generally. No spam networks, link farms, or low-quality directories are recommended anywhere in this document.

## Guiding Principle

Every link recommended here should come from a page a real UAE homeowner, business, or journalist would plausibly visit — not from a site whose only purpose is hosting outbound links. Quality and relevance over volume, every time.

---

## 1. Site Categories Worth Pursuing

### A. UAE Local Business Directories
Legitimate, well-established directories where a real UAE company listing is standard practice:
- Government/semi-government business directories (e.g., a Chamber of Commerce member directory, where AFAQ AL HAYAT is a genuine registered member).
- Established UAE business directory platforms with real editorial moderation (not auto-approved spam directories).
- Google Business Profile itself (already covered separately in `GOOGLE_ECOSYSTEM_FINAL_STATUS.md` — not duplicated here, but the single most important "directory" listing by far).

**Anchor text:** Company name ("AFAQ AL HAYAT") or a natural category phrase ("home maintenance and cleaning company in the UAE") — never an exact-match commercial keyword as anchor text on a directory listing (a spam signal).

### B. Real Estate and Property Platforms
UAE property portals and real-estate community sites often maintain "recommended service provider" or "vendor" sections for residents:
- Major UAE property listing portals' vendor/services directories, where available.
- Community management company partner/vendor lists for the specific communities in the approved Priority Community Registry (Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Hills Estate, Yas Island, etc.) — a natural fit since AFAQ already serves these areas.
- Property management company resource pages that link out to trusted maintenance/cleaning providers.

**Why this fits:** These are exactly the audiences searching for a home services provider, and the link context is topically perfect.

### C. Home Services / Trades Aggregator Sites
Legitimate UAE-focused home services marketplaces and aggregators (not lead-gen spam mills) that allow a business profile with a link back to the official site.

### D. Local News and Lifestyle Sites
UAE lifestyle and community publications that occasionally cover practical living topics (seasonal home care, pest patterns, moving to a new community) are realistic targets for a genuine guest contribution or expert-quote placement — see Guest Post Ideas below.

### E. Industry and Trade Associations
Any pest control, cleaning, or facilities-management industry association AFAQ AL HAYAT is or becomes a member of — membership directories are high-trust, low-effort links.

## What This Plan Explicitly Avoids
- Paid link networks or "guaranteed DA" link-selling services.
- Auto-approve directories with no editorial review (a well-known low-quality/spam signal).
- Reciprocal link-exchange schemes.
- Any site unrelated to home services, real estate, or UAE local business/community content.

## 4. Anchor Text Strategy

To avoid the exact-match anchor-text over-optimization pattern search engines penalize, anchor text across all backlink activity should follow this rough distribution:

| Anchor Type | Example | Target Share |
|---|---|---|
| Branded | "AFAQ AL HAYAT" | ~40% |
| Branded + descriptor | "AFAQ AL HAYAT's home maintenance team" | ~20% |
| Natural/generic | "this home maintenance guide," "read more here" | ~20% |
| Topical (not exact-match commercial) | "UAE pest control tips," "villa cleaning guide" | ~15% |
| Exact-match commercial | "pest control Dubai" | ~5%, only on the most contextually perfect, editorially-earned placements |

No two links from the same referring domain should use identical exact-match anchor text.

# Landing Page Standard

## Purpose

Define high-quality landing pages that can serve organic search, paid
campaigns, direct visitors, and returning customers without becoming disposable
advertising pages.

## Required Experience

Every landing page must:

- Match one clear user intent and approved service.
- State the service accurately above the fold.
- Explain scope, process, customer preparation, limitations, and next step.
- Use a verified contact or booking path.
- Provide trust evidence only when documented.
- Load quickly and work well on mobile.
- Meet accessibility and bilingual-content requirements.
- Remain useful when advertising is paused.

## Recommended Structure

1. Clear H1 and concise value proposition
2. Primary CTA and alternative contact route
3. Customer problem and suitability
4. Approved inclusions and exclusions
5. Delivery process
6. Safety or preparation guidance where applicable
7. Evidence, FAQs, and related resources
8. Transparent next step
9. Business/contact details from canonical sources

## Google Ads Alignment

- Advertisement, keyword intent, page title, H1, content, offer, and CTA must
  agree.
- Do not send all campaigns to the homepage.
- Do not create thin variants that differ only by keyword or location.
- Preserve clear navigation and business identity.
- Track meaningful outcomes and landing-page diagnostics separately.

## SEO and Indexing

An evergreen, unique, useful page may be indexable. A temporary or duplicated
campaign variant must follow an explicit canonical/noindex decision. URLs,
redirects, canonicals, language alternates, and internal links follow
`URL_AND_INTERNAL_LINKING_STANDARD.md`.

## Conversion Quality

Forms should request only necessary information, explain consent, validate
inputs, prevent spam, show a reliable success state, and create an auditable
handoff. Phone and WhatsApp actions must use verified details and track clicks
separately from qualified outcomes.

## Release Gate

- [ ] Approved facts and owner
- [ ] Message-to-page alignment
- [ ] Arabic and English review
- [ ] Mobile, speed, accessibility, and browser QA
- [ ] Forms, calls, WhatsApp, and booking tested
- [ ] Analytics and ad conversions validated
- [ ] SEO indexation/canonical decision recorded
- [ ] Privacy and consent reviewed
- [ ] Lead-routing owner ready

# SEO Strategy

## Document Information

- **Owner:** Marketing
- **Status:** Draft — measurement and owner approval required
- **Version:** 1.0

## Objective

Build discoverability from verified service knowledge and approved geographic
coverage while protecting users and search engines from duplicated, invented,
or unsupported claims.

## Strategy

1. Establish one indexable canonical page per approved service.
2. Create location pages only for approved service-location combinations.
3. Answer real customer questions through evidence-backed guides and FAQs.
4. Link website content to canonical service, brand, and market sources.
5. Publish Organization, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumb, Article, and local-business
   structured data only when their required facts are verified.
6. Measure qualified inquiries and bookings, not rankings alone.

## Publication Gates

- Service package is Approved.
- Contact and NAP records are Approved.
- Location is active in `03_MARKET/SERVICE_AREAS.md`.
- Arabic and English claims are equivalent.
- Safety, price, warranty, license, review, and response-time claims have
  evidence.
- Page has a unique purpose and is not a doorway-page variant.

## Measurement Framework

- Non-brand qualified organic sessions
- Search visibility for approved service topics
- Conversion to verified inquiry or booking
- Local profile actions where an eligible profile exists
- Indexed-page quality and duplication rate
- Technical health, Core Web Vitals, and crawl errors

Numeric baselines and targets require analytics access and owner approval.

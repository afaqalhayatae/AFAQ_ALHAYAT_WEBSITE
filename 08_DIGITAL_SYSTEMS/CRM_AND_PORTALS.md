# CRM, Customer Portal, and Owner Dashboard

## Document Control

- **Owner:** Business Owner
- **Status:** Draft — Workflow Approval Required
- **Version:** 0.1
- **Updated:** 2026-07-24

## CRM Boundary

The CRM records enquiries, consent, customer interactions, booking and quote
requests, outcomes, and approved follow-up. It must not invent customer facts,
prices, availability, staff, branches, or service commitments.

## Customer Portal Minimum

- secure access and session management;
- request and status visibility;
- consent and communication preferences;
- approved document and evidence exchange;
- correction, support, and owner-review escalation paths.

## Owner Dashboard Minimum

- pending approvals with risk and expiry;
- enquiries, bookings, incidents, and exceptions;
- agent and integration activity;
- content/publication state;
- audit evidence, reversibility, and recovery controls.

All customer-facing labels and flows require Arabic and English acceptance
criteria before release.

## Overview

Our painting service covers interior and exterior wall painting and touch-up work for homes and businesses across the UAE, from single-room refreshes to full-property repainting.

## Common Problems

- Faded, chipped, or peeling wall paint
- Visible stains, cracks, or patch marks on walls
- Color mismatch after a previous repair or patch
- Damp or moisture marks affecting paint finish
- Outdated color schemes needing a refresh
- Uneven or poor-quality previous paint work

## Scope

### Included

- Surface preparation (cleaning, minor filling, sanding)
- Interior wall and ceiling painting
- Exterior wall painting where accessible
- Primer application on new or repaired surfaces
- Color consultation based on the customer's preference
- Final surface cleanup after painting

### Excluded

- Structural repair of walls or ceilings
- Waterproofing or damp-proofing treatment (see our separate Waterproofing service)
- Specialist finishes requiring imported or custom materials
- Work at heights requiring specialized access equipment beyond standard reach

## Process Summary

1. Initial WhatsApp or phone consultation to understand the scope.
2. On-site assessment of the surfaces and area to be painted.
3. Color and finish discussion with the customer.
4. Surface preparation followed by primer and paint application.
5. Quality check of the finished surfaces with the customer.
6. Final cleanup of the work area.

## Why Choose Us

- Refreshed, consistent appearance across painted surfaces.
- Help covering stains, cracks, or patch marks evenly.
- Color guidance based on the space and the customer's preference.
- Scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- Clear explanation of the plan before any work starts.

## General Safety Notes

- Furniture and flooring are protected before painting begins.
- Work areas are kept ventilated during and after paint application.
- Any work requiring specialized access equipment is flagged to the customer rather than attempted without it.

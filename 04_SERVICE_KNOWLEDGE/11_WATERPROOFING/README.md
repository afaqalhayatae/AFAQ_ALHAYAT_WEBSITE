## Purpose

This package will become the authoritative definition of AFAQ Alhayat's
waterproofing service. The owner has confirmed that the service is offered
across the approved UAE coverage. “Waterproofing” currently means protection
against water ingress; thermal insulation is outside this package until the
owner confirms it as a separate scope.

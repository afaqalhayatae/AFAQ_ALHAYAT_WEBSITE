# General Cleaning Safety

## Status

Draft control set — competent safety review required before operational use.

## Primary Hazards

- Slips, trips, and wet floors
- Chemical exposure or incompatible products
- Manual handling and repetitive work
- Electrical equipment and wet environments
- Sharps, broken glass, biological material, or unknown substances
- Work at height or unsafe access
- Customer, child, pet, or public interaction
- Sensitive surfaces and property damage

## Minimum Controls

- Complete a task and site risk check.
- Use approved, labeled products with available safety information.
- Never mix products unless the approved manufacturer procedure permits it.
- Use task-appropriate PPE and ventilation.
- Isolate or clearly mark wet work areas.
- Inspect electrical tools and keep them away from unsafe wet conditions.
- Use safe lifting and approved access equipment.
- Stop and escalate specialist or hazardous conditions.
- Store, transport, and dispose of materials through approved processes.

## Incident Response

Protect people first, stop work, isolate the area, obtain appropriate emergency
support, notify the responsible supervisor, preserve evidence, and complete the
approved incident process. This document does not replace product instructions,
first-aid training, emergency services, or legal requirements.

## Customer Safety

Communicate access restrictions, ventilation, wet-floor risks, pet/child
controls, product sensitivities, and safe re-entry guidance based on the
approved method actually used.

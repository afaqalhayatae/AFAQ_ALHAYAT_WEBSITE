# General Cleaning Media Requirements

## Required Asset Types

- Service overview images
- Approved process and equipment images
- Preparation and safety illustrations
- Quality-check examples
- Short educational videos
- Arabic and English social variants

## Evidence and Rights

Every asset needs source, creator, date, rights/license, consent, location
privacy review, service ID, language, alt text, and approval status.

## Prohibited Representation

- Stock or generated media presented as real customer work
- Fabricated before/after results
- Visible customer identity, address, documents, or property details without
  consent
- Unsafe practices or unapproved products
- Unverified logos, certificates, ratings, or guarantees

## Delivery

Maintain an editable master and optimized derivatives. Use descriptive,
stable filenames and record where each asset is published.

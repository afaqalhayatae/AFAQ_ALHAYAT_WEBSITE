# General Cleaning FAQ

## What does General Cleaning include?

The exact tasks depend on the approved service scope. Routine accessible
surface cleaning may be included; specialist, restoration, hazardous, or
deep-cleaning tasks require separate confirmation.

## Is Deep Cleaning included?

No assumption should be made. Deep Cleaning is a separate service identity and
must have its own approved scope.

## Do you provide products and equipment?

The operating model is awaiting owner approval. The booking or work order must
state who supplies products and equipment.

## How long will the service take?

Duration depends on the approved areas, tasks, condition, access, team, and
constraints. No fixed duration should be promised without a validated model.

## Are products safe for children, pets, or sensitive people?

Suitability depends on the actual approved product, method, exposure, and
customer circumstances. Customers must disclose sensitivities, and the team
must follow labels, safety data, and approved controls.

## Do you guarantee stain removal?

No. Stain results depend on material, age, cause, prior treatment, and method.
No guarantee may be offered unless an approved written policy applies.

## Can I book recurring cleaning?

Recurring service may be offered only under approved availability, scope,
pricing, and contract rules.

## Which locations are served?

Only locations approved in `03_MARKET/SERVICE_AREAS.md` may be confirmed.

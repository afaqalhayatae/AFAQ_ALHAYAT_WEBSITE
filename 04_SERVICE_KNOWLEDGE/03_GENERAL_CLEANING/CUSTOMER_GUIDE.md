# General Cleaning Customer Guide

## Before the Visit

- Confirm the approved date, access method, and contact person.
- Secure valuables, cash, confidential documents, medicines, and fragile items.
- Identify sensitive surfaces, allergies, pets, children, and restricted areas.
- Remove personal clutter only where required by the agreed scope.
- Confirm parking, building access, permits, and utility availability.

## During the Service

- Keep children, pets, and unauthorized people away from active work areas.
- Raise any concern with the team lead rather than changing the scope directly
  with individual workers.
- Do not use wet or restricted areas until released.

## After the Service

- Review the agreed areas and report concerns promptly through the approved
  support channel.
- Follow any ventilation, drying, surface-care, or access guidance.
- Do not publish customer or worker images without consent.

## Important Limitations

Stain removal, restoration, disinfection, deep cleaning, post-construction
cleaning, hazardous waste, high-level work, and specialist upholstery/carpet
work are not automatically included.

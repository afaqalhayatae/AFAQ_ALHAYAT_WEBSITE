# General Cleaning Business Knowledge

## Document Information

- **Service ID:** `SVC-GENERAL-CLEANING`
- **Status:** Review Ready — commercial facts require owner approval
- **Owner:** Business and Operations

## Service Definition

General Cleaning is a routine cleaning service for agreed, accessible areas in
residential or commercial premises. The final task list is confirmed before
work begins and recorded in the service order.

## Customer Problems Addressed

- Routine dust, soil, and surface cleanliness
- Limited time or internal cleaning capacity
- Need for scheduled, documented cleaning
- Preparation for ordinary occupancy or business use

## Intended Segments

- Residential customers
- Offices and retail premises
- Property and facility managers
- Hospitality or other commercial customers only where the approved scope and
  operating controls are suitable

These are target segments, not proof of current eligibility or coverage.

## Value Proposition

The service should compete through clear scope, reliable coordination,
professional conduct, safe task execution, visible quality checks, and
documented follow-up—not unsupported claims of being the cheapest, fastest, or
best.

## Scope Governance

The quote or work order must identify premises, areas, tasks, exclusions,
frequency, customer preparation, access, special surfaces, risks, evidence,
acceptance method, and escalation contact.

## Commercial Inputs Required

- Package definitions and price factors
- Minimum booking or visit rules
- Staffing and duration model
- Cancellation and rescheduling terms
- Damage, rework, complaint, and warranty policy
- Contract and recurring-service options

# General Cleaning Training

## Training Outcomes

Personnel must be able to:

- Interpret the approved work order and boundaries.
- Identify common site and task hazards.
- Select and use approved tools, products, PPE, and signage.
- Prevent cross-contamination.
- Protect customer property and privacy.
- Perform the approved sequence and quality checks.
- Communicate professionally and escalate exceptions.
- Complete required records accurately.

## Assessment

Approval should require knowledge assessment, supervised demonstration,
scenario-based hazard response, quality inspection, and documented sign-off by
an authorized assessor.

## Refresher Triggers

- New product, equipment, surface, or procedure
- Incident, complaint, failed audit, or repeated rework
- Material legal or safety change
- Extended absence or expired competency

Training records must identify person, scope, assessor, result, date, expiry or
review trigger, and restrictions.

# General Cleaning Operations

## Status

Review Ready — this workflow requires operations and safety approval before
field use.

## Standard Workflow

1. Receive and validate the request.
2. Confirm address only through an approved booking channel.
3. Capture premises type, accessible areas, requested tasks, timing, and known
   constraints.
4. Decide whether a site assessment is required.
5. Issue and obtain acceptance of the service scope.
6. Assign an appropriately trained team and approved resources.
7. Conduct arrival, access, and dynamic risk checks.
8. Protect customer property and establish a logical cleaning sequence.
9. Perform the agreed tasks.
10. Conduct supervisor/self-inspection against the agreed acceptance criteria.
11. Record exceptions, damage, incomplete tasks, and evidence.
12. Obtain customer sign-off or documented remote confirmation.
13. Close, escalate, or schedule follow-up.

## Operational Controls

- Never expand scope without authorization.
- Follow product labels, surface-care instructions, and safety data.
- Use color coding or another approved method to prevent cross-contamination.
- Separate clean and used tools/materials.
- Stop and escalate when encountering sharps, biological contamination,
  hazardous substances, unstable access, electrical danger, or specialist work.
- Record pre-existing damage where relevant and permitted.

## Required Evidence

- Approved work order
- Arrival and completion timestamps
- Assigned team
- Exceptions and incident records
- Quality-check result
- Customer acceptance
- Photos only when authorized and privacy-safe

## Owner Input Required

Approved task sequence, productivity assumptions, staffing ratios, equipment
list, material list, inspection sampling, and rework threshold.

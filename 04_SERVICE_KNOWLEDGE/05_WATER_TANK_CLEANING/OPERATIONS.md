# Water Tank Cleaning Operations

## Status

Review Ready — not approved for field execution until safety and compliance
sign-off.

## Controlled Workflow

1. Validate request, tank use, location, type, material, capacity, access, and
   known condition.
2. Determine site assessment, permits, building coordination, supply
   interruption, and testing requirements.
3. Approve method statement, risk assessment, resources, isolation, waste, and
   release criteria.
4. Confirm customer authorization and affected-user communication.
5. Identify the tank and utilities; isolate through an approved process.
6. Drain or manage stored water through an approved route.
7. Establish safe access and determine whether entry is permitted.
8. Remove accessible debris/deposits and clean approved surfaces.
9. Apply only the approved hygiene/disinfection method where authorized.
10. Rinse, inspect, and complete required verification.
11. Refill/recommission only under the approved responsibility model.
12. Record evidence, exceptions, products, measurements, testing, and release.
13. Obtain sign-off and communicate aftercare or restrictions.

## Stop Conditions

Stop for unapproved entry, oxygen/atmospheric concern, unsafe access,
unidentified utilities, structural damage, hazardous contamination,
uncontrolled discharge, missing isolation, unsuitable products, or any
condition beyond capability.

## Required Records

Work order, tank identity, assessment, permits, isolation, team competency,
products and batch data, method parameters, waste route, inspection, test
results when applicable, release authorization, photos with consent, and
customer sign-off.

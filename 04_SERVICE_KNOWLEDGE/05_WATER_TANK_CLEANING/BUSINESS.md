# Water Tank Cleaning Business Knowledge

## Status

Review Ready — commercial and compliance facts require approval.

## Service Definition

Controlled cleaning of an approved water-storage tank following an assessed
scope, safe isolation, removal of accessible deposits, the approved hygiene
method, inspection, documentation, and release criteria.

## Intended Customers

Residential, commercial, property-management, hospitality, healthcare,
education, or other facilities only where the tank, access, method, and
regulatory requirements fall within the approved capability.

## Value

The service should create value through documented inspection, controlled
execution, traceability, responsible hygiene practice, clear limitations, and
evidence—not unconditional claims that water is safe to drink or free from
microorganisms.

## Commercial Inputs Required

- Assessment and quotation method
- Tank-type and size factors
- Access, isolation, drainage, refill, and downtime responsibilities
- Testing and laboratory options
- Recurring schedule and contract terms
- Rework, cancellation, damage, and warranty rules

# Water Tank Cleaning FAQ

## Does cleaning guarantee safe drinking water?

No unconditional guarantee is made. Safety depends on the full water system,
source, tank condition, method, refill, ongoing contamination controls, and any
required testing or authority criteria.

## Is disinfection always included?

Only when specified in the approved scope using an approved product and method.

## Do technicians enter the tank?

Not automatically. Entry requires a competent assessment and a complete
approved confined-space system. Non-entry methods should be considered where
appropriate.

## Is water testing included?

Only when the work order defines sampling responsibility, method, laboratory,
parameters, timing, acceptance criteria, and reporting.

## How often should a tank be cleaned?

Frequency must follow applicable requirements, tank risk, inspection,
manufacturer/property policy, water quality, and owner-approved guidance. No
universal frequency is asserted here.

## Which tank types are supported?

The approved capability matrix is still required. Tank material, configuration,
size, use, access, and condition must be assessed.

## Which locations are served?

Only locations approved in `03_MARKET/SERVICE_AREAS.md` may be confirmed.

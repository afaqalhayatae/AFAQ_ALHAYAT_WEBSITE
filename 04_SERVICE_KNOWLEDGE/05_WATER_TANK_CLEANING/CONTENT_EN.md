## Title

Water Tank Cleaning

## Short Description

Controlled inspection and cleaning of supported water-storage tanks, delivered
through an agreed scope, documented safety controls, inspection, and release
process.

## Overview

Each tank is assessed for use, type, access, condition, isolation, drainage,
method, verification, and customer responsibilities. Cleaning, disinfection,
testing, and certification are separate deliverables unless explicitly
included.

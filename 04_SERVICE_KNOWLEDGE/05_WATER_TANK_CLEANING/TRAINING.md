# Water Tank Cleaning Training

## Required Competency Areas

- Tank identification, inspection, scope, and limitations
- Risk assessment, isolation, safe access, and stop-work decisions
- Confined-space awareness and role-specific authorization
- Product labels, safety data, dilution/contact controls where applicable
- Cleaning equipment, hygiene, cross-contamination, and waste
- Inspection, test/sample boundaries, release, and records
- Customer communication, privacy, and incident escalation

## Authorization

Training attendance alone does not authorize tank entry, chemical application,
sampling, testing, electrical isolation, work at height, or rescue. Each
high-risk task requires documented role competency and approval.

## Assessment

Use knowledge tests, practical demonstration, scenario response, supervised
work, record review, and formal sign-off. Refresh after incidents, failed
audits, new methods/products/equipment, regulatory change, or competency expiry.

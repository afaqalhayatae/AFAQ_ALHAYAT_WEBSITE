# Water Tank Cleaning Customer Guide

## Before Service

- Confirm the correct tank, building contact, access, and affected users.
- Agree water interruption, drainage, refill, and equipment responsibilities.
- Disclose known damage, contamination, prior treatment, access restrictions,
  and sensitive occupants or operations.
- Store sufficient water only if advised through an approved plan.
- Prevent use of the system during isolation and restricted periods.

## During Service

Keep unauthorized people away. Do not operate valves, pumps, covers, or supply
controls unless directed by the responsible person.

## After Service

Use the system only after formal release. Follow flushing, refill, testing,
ventilation, or temporary-use restrictions in the approved completion record.
Report unusual odor, color, leakage, damage, or illness through the appropriate
channel and seek qualified assistance where needed.

## Important Limitation

Cleaning appearance alone does not prove drinking-water safety. Laboratory
testing, sampling, certification, and authority acceptance apply only when
explicitly included and performed through an approved process.

# Water Tank Cleaning Media Requirements

## Required Assets

- Tank-type and access illustrations
- Safe isolation and work-zone examples
- Approved equipment and process images
- Customer preparation and release guidance
- Inspection and documentation examples
- Arabic and English educational video variants

## Restrictions

Do not show unsafe entry, missing fall protection, unlabeled products,
uncontrolled discharge, customer addresses, building identifiers, test results,
or claims of purity/safety without evidence. Stock or generated images must not
be presented as actual completed work.

## Asset Records

Record source, creator, date, consent, rights, service ID, tank context, safety
review, generation method, language, alt text, approval, and publication use.

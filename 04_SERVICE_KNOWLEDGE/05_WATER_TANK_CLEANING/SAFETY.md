# Water Tank Cleaning Safety

## Critical Hazards

- Confined or restricted space
- Oxygen deficiency or hazardous atmosphere
- Falls, ladders, roof access, and tank openings
- Drowning, engulfment, or unexpected inflow
- Electrical and pump systems
- Chemical exposure and incompatible products
- Slips, manual handling, biological contamination, and waste
- Service interruption and unintended use before release

## Mandatory Control Principle

No person enters a tank unless a competent assessment confirms entry is
necessary and an approved confined-space system is in place, including permit,
isolation, atmospheric testing, ventilation, communications, attendant,
rescue capability, trained personnel, and suitable equipment.

## Minimum Controls

- Verified isolation and prevention of unexpected inflow/energization
- Approved access and fall protection
- Task-specific PPE and hygiene controls
- Labeled products, safety data, approved dilution/contact method
- Segregated work area and customer/public communication
- Controlled discharge and waste handling
- Emergency and rescue plan appropriate to the work
- Stop-work authority for every team member

This document does not approve tank entry, a product, a concentration, a
disinfection claim, or a regulatory method.

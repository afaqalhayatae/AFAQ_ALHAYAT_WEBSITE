## Service Title

Electrical Maintenance

## Short Description

Safe, reliable electrical repairs for your home or business, wherever you are in the UAE.

## Overview

Our electrical maintenance service addresses everyday household and commercial electrical faults — from faulty switches and sockets to lighting and distribution-panel issues — across UAE properties.

## Common Problems

- Sockets or switches that don't work or feel loose
- Lights flickering, dimming, or not turning on
- A breaker that trips repeatedly
- Buzzing or unusual sounds from a panel or fixture
- Partial power loss to a room or area
- Old or damaged wiring visible at a fixture

## Scope

### Included

- Switch and socket inspection and repair
- Lighting fixture troubleshooting and repair
- Distribution-panel and breaker inspection
- Fault-finding for partial power-loss issues
- Replacement of damaged fittings and accessories
- General electrical safety inspection

### Excluded

- Full property rewiring or new circuit installation
- Utility-provider meter or main-supply work
- Manufacturer warranty claims on appliances
- Any work beyond what can be safely assessed without specialized regulatory sign-off

## Process Summary

1. Initial WhatsApp or phone consultation to describe the electrical issue.
2. On-site inspection of the affected circuit, fixture, or panel.
3. Diagnosis explained to the customer before work begins.
4. Agreed repair or maintenance work carried out.
5. Circuit or fixture tested to confirm the fault is resolved.
6. Final walkthrough with safety recommendations for the customer.

## Why Choose Us

- Faster, more accurate diagnosis of the actual electrical fault.
- Reduced risk from unresolved wiring or fixture issues over time.
- Support for both minor repairs and routine electrical upkeep.
- Scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- Clear explanation of findings before any work starts.

## General Safety Notes

- Power to the affected circuit is isolated before inspection or repair work.
- Technicians follow standard electrical safety practice throughout every visit.
- Any fault outside routine maintenance scope, or requiring specialized regulatory sign-off, is flagged to the customer rather than attempted.

# Electrical Maintenance Safety

## Document Information

- **Service ID:** `SVC-ELECTRICAL-MAINTENANCE`
- **Status:** Draft — Competent Safety Review Required


## Safety Boundary

This file is a safety-review register, not a method statement or field
instruction. Work must not be authorized from this draft.

## Evidence Required

- Service-specific hazard assessment
- Competency and authorization requirements
- Approved controls, isolation rules, and stop-work criteria
- PPE selection based on assessed hazards
- Product or equipment safety documents where applicable
- Incident, emergency, waste, and environmental procedures
- Customer, occupant, property, and public-protection controls

## Mandatory Escalation

If scope, competency, hazard, control, or legal responsibility is unclear, stop
and escalate for owner and competent review.

## Evidence Gate

These facts require owner confirmation or competent evidence before approval:

- included and excluded scope;
- operational methods, tools, materials, and resources;
- competency, safety, compliance, and escalation requirements;
- duration, availability, commercial terms, price, and warranty;
- performance, quality, licensing, and regulatory claims.

Unknown facts remain `Pending Owner Input`; they must not be inferred from a
service name, generic industry practice, or a source draft.

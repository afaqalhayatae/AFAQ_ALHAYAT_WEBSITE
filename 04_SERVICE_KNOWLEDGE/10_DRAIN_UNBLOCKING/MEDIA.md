# Drain Unblocking Media Requirements

## Document Information

- **Service ID:** `SVC-DRAIN-UNBLOCKING`
- **Status:** Draft Asset Brief


## Required Asset Classes

- Service overview and customer-journey imagery
- Privacy-safe work evidence
- Before/after assets only where authentic, comparable, and approved
- Bilingual icons or diagrams where they improve understanding

## Evidence and Rights

Each asset requires source, consent/rights, capture date, service ID, language,
alt text, privacy review, factual-caption review, and approval status.

## Prohibited Representation

- Staged work presented as a completed customer job
- Unverified tools, products, uniforms, vehicles, credentials, or methods
- Fabricated results, reviews, ratings, certifications, or authority logos
- AI-generated images presented as documentary evidence

## Evidence Gate

These facts require owner confirmation or competent evidence before approval:

- included and excluded scope;
- operational methods, tools, materials, and resources;
- competency, safety, compliance, and escalation requirements;
- duration, availability, commercial terms, price, and warranty;
- performance, quality, licensing, and regulatory claims.

Unknown facts remain `Pending Owner Input`; they must not be inferred from a
service name, generic industry practice, or a source draft.

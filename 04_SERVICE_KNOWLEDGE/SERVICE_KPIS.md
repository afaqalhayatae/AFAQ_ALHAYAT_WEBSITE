# Service KPIs

## Purpose

Cross-service quality and performance indicators, so every service package measures success consistently. Individual services may track additional service-specific KPIs inside their own package.

Derived from [`01_BUSINESS/BUSINESS_GOALS.md`](../01_BUSINESS/BUSINESS_GOALS.md) ("Success Indicators") and `99_STANDARDS/SERVICE_TEMPLATE.md` ("KPIs") — both existing, approved sources.

---

# Cross-Service KPIs

- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- First-Time Resolution Rate
- Response Time (request to scheduled visit)
- Repeat Service / Retention Rate
- Complaint Rate
- On-Time Arrival Rate
- Positive Google Review Rate
- Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Growth

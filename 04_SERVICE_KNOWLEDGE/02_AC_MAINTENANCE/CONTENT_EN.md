## Service Title

AC Maintenance

## Short Description

Keep every room comfortable — professional AC servicing across the UAE, one call away.

## Overview

Our AC maintenance service keeps split, window, and central air conditioning units running efficiently across UAE homes and businesses, covering routine servicing, common performance issues, and preventive upkeep suited to the region's demanding climate.

## Common Problems

- Weak or reduced cooling airflow
- Unusual noises, vibration, or rattling from the unit
- Water leaking or dripping from the indoor unit
- Persistent bad odors when the AC is running
- Electricity bills that seem high relative to AC use
- A unit that won't turn on or shuts off unexpectedly

## Scope

### Included

- Filter inspection and cleaning
- Coil and drain-line inspection
- Visual check of refrigerant lines and connections
- Thermostat and control-settings check
- Indoor and outdoor unit cleaning
- Performance assessment with a summary of findings

### Excluded

- Major refrigerant-system repairs requiring specialized parts
- Structural or ducting installation work
- Manufacturer warranty claims on the AC unit itself
- Electrical rewiring beyond the AC unit's own connections

## Process Summary

1. Initial WhatsApp or phone consultation to understand the issue.
2. On-site inspection of the unit and its installation.
3. Diagnosis shared with the customer before any work begins.
4. Agreed maintenance work carried out after customer approval.
5. Unit tested to confirm improved performance.
6. Final walkthrough with care recommendations for the customer.

## Why Choose Us

- Improved cooling performance and indoor comfort.
- Support in reducing electricity use linked to AC inefficiency.
- Helps extend the working life of existing AC equipment.
- Scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- Clear explanation of findings before any work starts.

## General Safety Notes

- AC units involve both electrical components and refrigerant; technicians follow standard safe-handling practice for both.
- Any issue outside routine maintenance scope is flagged to the customer rather than attempted without the right expertise.
- Work areas are left clean and unused parts/materials are removed after service.

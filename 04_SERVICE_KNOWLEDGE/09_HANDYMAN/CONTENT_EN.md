## Service Title

Handyman Services

## Short Description

One call for the small jobs that pile up — general handyman service across the UAE.

## Overview

Our handyman service covers small, general repair and installation tasks around the home or office that don't need a dedicated trade specialist — the kind of everyday jobs that can otherwise sit unresolved on a to-do list.

## Common Problems

- A door or cabinet that doesn't close properly
- Loose hinges, handles, or fittings
- Furniture assembly or minor furniture repair
- Small wall-mounting jobs (shelves, mirrors, curtain rods)
- Minor carpentry touch-ups
- General small repairs that don't fit a single trade category

## Scope

### Included

- Door, cabinet, and hinge adjustment
- Furniture assembly and minor repair
- Wall-mounting of shelves, mirrors, and fixtures
- Minor carpentry and fitting repairs
- General small-item installation
- Multi-task visits combining several small jobs in one call-out

### Excluded

- Specialized trade work (major electrical, plumbing, or AC repair — see our dedicated services)
- Structural carpentry or renovation-scale work
- Manufacturer warranty claims on furniture or fittings
- Any job requiring a licensed trade specialist beyond general handyman scope

## Process Summary

1. Initial WhatsApp or phone consultation describing the task(s).
2. On-site assessment of the job or jobs to be done.
3. Confirmation of scope with the customer before work begins.
4. Work carried out, often covering several small tasks in one visit.
5. Quality check of completed work with the customer.
6. Work area left clean at the end of the visit.

## Why Choose Us

- One visit can often cover several small, unrelated jobs at once.
- Useful for tasks too small for a single dedicated trade call-out.
- Convenient for tenants and homeowners clearing a maintenance backlog.
- Scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- Clear scope agreed with the customer before work starts.

## General Safety Notes

- Any task found to require a licensed trade specialist is flagged to the customer rather than attempted outside scope.
- Tools and materials are handled following standard safe-practice for general repair work.
- Work areas are left tidy and hazard-free at the end of the visit.

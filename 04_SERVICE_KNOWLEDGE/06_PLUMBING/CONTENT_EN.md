## Service Title

Plumbing

## Short Description

From a dripping tap to a stubborn leak — plumbing help across the UAE, done right.

## Overview

Our plumbing service covers everyday repairs and maintenance for the pipes, fittings, and fixtures found in UAE homes and businesses — from leaking taps to blocked or slow-draining fixtures.

## Common Problems

- Leaking or dripping taps and mixers
- Low water pressure at a sink, shower, or tap
- Running or noisy toilet cisterns
- Slow-draining sinks, showers, or bathtubs
- Visible pipe leaks under sinks or along exposed piping
- Water heater connection issues

## Scope

### Included

- Tap, mixer, and fixture leak repair
- Pipe joint and fitting inspection and repair
- Toilet cistern and flush mechanism repair
- Water heater connection checks
- Drain and trap inspection at sinks and showers
- General plumbing fault diagnosis

### Excluded

- Major re-piping or full bathroom re-plumbing projects
- Main water-line or municipal connection work
- Manufacturer warranty claims on fixtures or appliances
- Structural work to access hidden piping

## Process Summary

1. Initial WhatsApp or phone consultation to describe the issue.
2. On-site inspection of the affected fixture or pipework.
3. Diagnosis explained to the customer before work begins.
4. Agreed repair or maintenance work carried out.
5. Fixture tested for leaks and correct function.
6. Work area left clean with a summary of what was done.

## Why Choose Us

- Faster identification of the actual source of a leak or blockage.
- Reduced water waste from unresolved leaks over time.
- Support for both minor repairs and routine plumbing upkeep.
- Scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- Clear explanation of findings before any work starts.

## General Safety Notes

- Water supply to the affected fixture is isolated before repair work where needed.
- Technicians check for related issues (e.g. water damage) while addressing the reported fault.
- Any issue beyond routine plumbing scope is flagged to the customer rather than attempted without the right expertise.

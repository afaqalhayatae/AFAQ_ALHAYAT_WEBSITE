# Customer Guide

## Purpose

This guide helps customers understand the Pest Control service before, during, and after treatment.

---

# Before the Visit

- Confirm your appointment.
- Keep children and pets away from treatment areas.
- Cover or store exposed food.
- Inform the technician about any allergies or special conditions.
- Provide access to all affected areas.

---

# During the Service

- Follow the technician's instructions.
- Stay away from treatment areas if requested.
- Do not touch equipment or chemicals.
- Ask any questions before the technician leaves.

---

# After the Service

- Follow the recommended re-entry time.
- Do not clean treated areas immediately unless instructed.
- Keep monitoring for pest activity.
- Follow all prevention recommendations.

---

# Prevention Tips

- Keep the property clean.
- Store food in sealed containers.
- Repair water leaks.
- Dispose of waste regularly.
- Seal cracks and entry points.

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Purpose

This document contains the most common customer questions and their approved answers.

---

## Is the treatment safe?

Safety depends on the actual pest, site assessment, approved product and method,
occupant conditions, and compliance with the product label and competent
safety controls. The company must not give an unconditional safety assurance.

---

## Is it safe for children and pets?

This requires case-specific assessment and approved product guidance. Customers
must disclose children, pets, allergies, pregnancy, or other sensitive
conditions before service. No unconditional assurance is approved.

---

## How long does the treatment take?

The duration depends on the property size and the type of infestation. The technician will provide an estimated time before starting.

---

## When can I return to the treated area?

Your technician will advise the recommended re-entry time based on the treatment performed.

---

## Will one treatment solve the problem?

Some infestations are resolved with one visit, while others may require follow-up treatments or a maintenance plan.

---

## What pests do you treat?

Our service covers:

- Cockroaches
- Ants
- Bed Bugs
- Rodents
- Termites
- Mosquitoes
- Flies
- Fleas
- Other common pests

---

## Do you offer warranty?

Warranty terms are pending owner approval and must be confirmed in the written
quotation or service order. No warranty should be assumed from this FAQ.

---

## How can I prevent pests from returning?

- Keep the property clean.
- Remove food and water sources.
- Seal entry points.
- Schedule regular inspections.

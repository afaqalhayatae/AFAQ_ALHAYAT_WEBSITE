# Operations

## Purpose

This document defines the operational procedures for delivering the Pest Control service safely, consistently, and professionally.

The workflow is a draft structure only. Treatment methods, tools, products,
inspection criteria, competence requirements, safety controls, service
duration, and acceptance criteria require owner and competent technical review.

---

# Service Workflow

1. Receive Customer Request
2. Schedule Appointment
3. Site Visit
4. Inspection
5. Pest Identification
6. Risk Assessment
7. Treatment Planning
8. Customer Approval
9. Service Execution
10. Quality Inspection
11. Customer Education
12. Service Report
13. Follow-up

---

# Inspection Checklist

- Entry Points
- Pest Activity
- Nest Locations
- Food Sources
- Water Sources
- Structural Damage
- Waste Areas
- Outdoor Environment

---

# Treatment Methods

- Gel Treatment
- Residual Spray
- Dust Application
- Rodent Bait Stations
- Traps
- ULV Fogging
- Fumigation (when required)

---

# Required Equipment

- Sprayers
- ULV Fogger
- Gel Applicator
- Dust Applicator
- Flashlight
- Inspection Mirror
- PPE Kit

---

# Completion Checklist

- Treatment Completed
- Customer Informed
- Safety Instructions Provided
- Photos Taken
- Report Completed
- Follow-up Scheduled (if required)

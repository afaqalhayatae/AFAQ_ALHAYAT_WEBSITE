- Bright, natural daylight or clean professional interior lighting. No harsh shadows, no artificial color casts.

## UAE Environment

- Recognizable UAE residential or commercial context (villa, apartment building, or commercial unit) without depicting any real, identifiable private property or third-party branding.

- Neat, professional, consistent with a UAE home-services technician — matches the same team identity used across all AFAQ AL HAYAT services.

- Same uniform style/color scheme across all services and all images. No invented certifications or badges on uniforms.

- Official logo only, sourced from approved brand assets. No recreated, approximated, or AI-generated logo marks.

- No fake logos or invented brand marks.
- No invented phone numbers, addresses, or license/certification badges.
- No incorrect Arabic or English spelling.

- No fake customer identities or fabricated reviews.

## Quality Requirements

- Desktop and mobile optimized, sharp focus, no visible compression artifacts, no watermarks.

# Service Packages

## Purpose

This document defines the official service packages offered by AFAQ Alhayat to simplify customer decisions, increase average order value, and support long-term customer relationships.

---

# Package Overview

AFAQ Alhayat offers flexible service packages designed for residential and commercial customers with different maintenance needs and budgets.

---

# Basic Package

Suitable For

- Apartments
- Small Offices

Includes

- General Inspection
- Basic Maintenance
- Minor Repairs
- Standard Customer Support

---

# Standard Package

Suitable For

- Villas
- Medium Businesses

Includes

- Scheduled Maintenance
- Priority Booking
- Preventive Inspection
- Discount on Additional Services
- Service Reports

---

# Premium Package

Suitable For

- Large Villas
- Commercial Buildings
- Hotels
- Property Management Companies

Includes

- Full Preventive Maintenance
- Emergency Priority Response
- Dedicated Account Support
- Quarterly Service Reports
- Annual Maintenance Planning
- Exclusive Service Discounts

- Reduced emergency repairs

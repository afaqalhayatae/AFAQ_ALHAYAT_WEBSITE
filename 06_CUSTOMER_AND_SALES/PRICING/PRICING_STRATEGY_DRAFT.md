## Purpose

This document defines the pricing strategy for AFAQ Alhayat to ensure competitive positioning, profitability, transparency, and customer satisfaction.

---

# Pricing Philosophy

AFAQ Alhayat follows a value-based pricing strategy that balances:

- Service Quality
- Professionalism
- Customer Value
- Market Competitiveness
- Long-Term Relationships

---

# Pricing Principles

- Transparent pricing
- No hidden charges
- Fair market rates
- Consistent quotations
- Professional estimation process

---

# Pricing Models

## Fixed Price

Suitable for:

- Standard cleaning
- Water tank cleaning
- Pest control packages

---

## Inspection-Based Pricing

Suitable for:

- AC maintenance
- Plumbing
- Electrical work
- Painting projects
- Major repairs

---

## Hourly Pricing

Suitable for:

- Handyman services
- Small repair jobs
- Installation services

---

## Contract Pricing

Suitable for:

- Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
- Commercial maintenance
- Property management
- Corporate clients

---

# Pricing Factors

Prices may vary based on:

- Property size
- Service complexity
- Number of technicians
- Required materials
- Emergency requests
- Service location
- Contract duration

---

# Discounts

Available for:

- Annual contracts
- Multiple services
- Corporate clients
- Property management companies
- Seasonal promotions

---

# Quotation Process

1. Customer inquiry
2. Service assessment
3. Site inspection (if required)
4. Price calculation
5. Official quotation
6. Customer approval
7. Service scheduling

---

# Customer Commitment

Every quotation should be:

- Clear
- Accurate
- Transparent
- Competitive
- Easy to understand

---

# Business Objective

The pricing strategy aims to:

- Build customer trust
- Increase conversion rates
- Support sustainable growth
- Maintain healthy profit margins
- Encourage long-term customer relationships

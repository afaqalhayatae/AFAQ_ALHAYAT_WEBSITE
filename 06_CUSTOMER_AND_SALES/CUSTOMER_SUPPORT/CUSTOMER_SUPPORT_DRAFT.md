## Purpose

This document defines the customer support standards, communication channels, response procedures, and service expectations for AFAQ Alhayat.

---

# Customer Support Mission

Deliver fast, professional, and customer-focused support that builds trust, resolves issues efficiently, and creates long-term customer relationships.

---

# Support Channels

Customers can contact AFAQ Alhayat through:

- Website Contact Form
- Online Booking Form
- WhatsApp Business
- Phone Calls
- Email
- Google Business Profile
- Social Media

---

# Support Availability

Support hours are `Owner Input Required`.

Urgent requests must enter owner-review escalation unless approved emergency
availability and rules exist in a canonical source.

---

# Response Standards

Website Inquiry

- Respond as quickly as possible.

WhatsApp

- Acknowledge messages promptly.

Phone Calls

- Answer professionally and identify the company.

Email

- Reply with clear and complete information.

---

# Customer Support Workflow

1. Receive customer inquiry.
2. Verify customer details.
3. Understand the issue or request.
4. Route to the responsible governed agent role or the owner-review escalation queue.
5. Provide updates until resolution.
6. Confirm customer satisfaction.
7. Close the support request.

---

# Customer Communication Principles

Every interaction should be:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Respectful
- Honest
- Solution-Oriented
- Clear

---

# Complaint Handling

When a complaint is received:

1. Listen carefully.
2. Apologize when appropriate.
3. Investigate the issue.
4. Offer a practical solution.
5. Follow up after resolution.
6. Record the case for future improvement.

---

# Customer Satisfaction

Success should be measured through:

- Customer feedback
- Google Reviews
- Repeat bookings
- Referral customers
- Resolution time

---

# Continuous Improvement

Customer support should continuously improve by:

- Refining AI agent instructions and knowledge sources
- Reviewing feedback
- Updating procedures
- Improving response times
- Using AI-assisted support tools

# Upsell & Cross-Sell Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the upsell and cross-sell strategy for AFAQ Alhayat to increase customer value while providing additional services that genuinely benefit customers.

---

# Objectives

The strategy aims to:

- Increase average order value.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Offer complete maintenance solutions.
- Strengthen long-term customer relationships.
- Increase recurring revenue.

---

# Upselling

Upselling encourages customers to upgrade to a higher-value service.

Examples:

- Upgrade from Basic Cleaning to Deep Cleaning.
- Upgrade to an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC).
- Upgrade to a Premium Service Package.
- Add Emergency Priority Support.

---

# Cross-Selling

Cross-selling recommends complementary services.

Examples:

After AC Maintenance:

- Air Duct Cleaning
- Electrical Inspection

After Plumbing:

- Water Tank Cleaning
- Bathroom Deep Cleaning

After Pest Control:

- Deep Cleaning
- Preventive Maintenance

After Painting:

- Handyman Services
- Electrical Inspection

---

# Recommendation Rules

Recommendations should always be:

- Relevant
- Helpful
- Honest
- Based on customer needs
- Never aggressive

---

# Customer Journey

Suitable recommendation points include:

- During quotation
- Before service completion
- After service completion
- During follow-up calls
- During AMC renewal

---

# Digital Recommendations

The website should display:

- Related Services
- Recommended Packages
- Annual Maintenance Plans
- Frequently Booked Together
- Customer Testimonials

---

# CRM Opportunities

The CRM should record:

- Customer service history
- Previous bookings
- Preferred services
- Recommended future services
- Renewal opportunities

---

# Success Metrics

Measure:

- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Cross-Sell Conversion Rate
- Upsell Conversion Rate
- Repeat Customer Rate
- AMC Conversion Rate
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

---

# Business Objective

Upselling and cross-selling should always improve the customer's experience by offering valuable, relevant services while supporting sustainable business growth.

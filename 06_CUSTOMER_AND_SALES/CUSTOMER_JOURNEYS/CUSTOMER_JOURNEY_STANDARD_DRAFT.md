# Customer Journey Standard

## Document Control

- Owner: Business owner
- Status: Draft
- Version: 0.1
- Publication: Blocked

## Journey Record

Each approved journey must identify:

- persona and verified need;
- entry channel and consent/privacy requirements;
- governed agent responsible for each digital step;
- owner-review and external-provider handoffs;
- canonical sources for service, coverage, contact, price, and warranty facts;
- failure states, escalation, evidence, and measurable outcome.

## Publication Gate

No journey may promise availability, response time, price, warranty, payment
method, provider assignment, or service coverage beyond an approved canonical
source.

# Executive Summary

The Homepage is the primary digital entry point of AFAQ Alhayat. It is designed to establish trust, communicate the company's value proposition, highlight core services, support AI discoverability, and maximize visitor conversion into qualified leads and bookings.

---

# Business Objectives

- Build instant trust.
- Present the company professionally.
- Increase online bookings.
- Improve conversion rate.
- Support Local SEO.
- Optimize AI discoverability.
- Guide visitors through a clear customer journey.

# Safety

## Purpose

This document defines the health, safety, and environmental requirements for the Pest Control service.

---

# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

- Safety Gloves
- Safety Goggles
- Respirator Mask
- Protective Coveralls
- Safety Shoes

---

# Before Service

- Inspect the work area.
- Inform the customer about the treatment.
- Protect food and sensitive items.
- Keep children and pets away.

---

# During Service

- Wear all required PPE.
- Follow product instructions.
- Avoid food preparation areas unless required.
- Use only approved chemicals.

---

# After Service

- Dispose of waste safely.
- Clean all equipment.
- Inform the customer of re-entry time.
- Provide after-service instructions.

---

# Emergency Procedures

- Chemical spill response.
- First aid procedures.
- Emergency contact numbers.
- Incident reporting.

---

# Safety Rules

- Never mix chemicals unless approved.
- Never eat or drink during treatment.
- Store chemicals securely.
- Follow municipality regulations.
- Report all incidents immediately.

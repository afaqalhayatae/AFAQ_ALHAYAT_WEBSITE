# Training

## Purpose

This document defines the minimum training requirements for technicians performing the Pest Control service.

---

# Required Knowledge

- Pest identification
- Treatment methods
- Chemical safety
- Equipment operation
- Customer communication
- Municipality regulations

---

# Required Skills

- Site inspection
- Risk assessment
- Safe chemical application
- Report writing
- Problem solving
- Professional customer interaction

---

# Required Certifications

- Pest Control Training
- Health & Safety Training
- Chemical Handling Training
- First Aid (Recommended)

---

# Training Checklist

- Understand service workflow
- Perform inspection correctly
- Select the proper treatment
- Use PPE correctly
- Complete service reports
- Explain after-service instructions to customers

---

# Performance Evaluation

Technicians should be evaluated on:

- Technical knowledge
- Service quality
- Safety compliance
- Customer satisfaction
- Documentation accuracy

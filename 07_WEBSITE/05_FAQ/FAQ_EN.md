## Maintenance

### AC Maintenance

**1. How often should an AC unit be maintained?**
This depends on usage patterns, unit type, and environment. Many households in the UAE have units checked more frequently around peak summer months; a technician can recommend a suitable schedule after inspecting your specific unit.

**2. Why is my AC unit making unusual noises?**
Unusual noises can have several causes — loose parts, debris, fan issues, or a mounting problem, among others. A technician needs to inspect the unit directly to identify the actual cause before recommending a fix.

**3. Does AC maintenance come with a warranty?**
Warranty terms are subject to company policy — please confirm directly with our team before the visit.

**4. How much does AC maintenance cost?**
Pricing depends on the number of units, their condition, and the work required. This is subject to company policy — please contact us directly for an accurate quote for your property.

## Cleaning

### General Cleaning

**20. How often should I book general cleaning?**
This depends on household size, occupancy, and preference — many customers book weekly or bi-weekly visits, while others prefer occasional one-time cleanings.

**21. What's the difference between general cleaning and deep cleaning?**
General cleaning covers routine upkeep (dusting, floors, kitchen/bathroom surfaces), while deep cleaning is a more intensive session covering areas not part of routine cleaning, such as grout or behind appliances.

## Pest Control

**26. Is pest control treatment safe?**
Safety depends on the specific pest, a site assessment, the approved product and method used, occupant conditions, and compliance with the product label and safety controls. AFAQ AL HAYAT does not give an unconditional safety assurance.

**27. Is pest control safe for children and pets?**
This requires case-specific assessment and approved product guidance. Customers must disclose children, pets, allergies, pregnancy, or other sensitive conditions before service — there is no unconditional assurance.

**28. How long does pest control treatment take?**
Duration depends on the property size and the type of infestation. The technician provides an estimated time before starting.

**29. When can I return to a treated area?**
Your technician advises the recommended re-entry time based on the specific treatment performed.

**30. Will one treatment solve a pest problem?**
Some infestations are resolved with one visit; others may need follow-up treatments or a maintenance plan.

**31. What pests does AFAQ AL HAYAT treat?**
Cockroaches, ants, bed bugs, rodents, termites, mosquitoes, flies, fleas, and other common pests.

**32. Does AFAQ AL HAYAT offer a warranty on pest control?**
Warranty details are confirmed in your written quotation or service order, so you know exactly what's covered before work begins.

**33. How can I prevent pests from returning?**
Keep the property clean, remove food and water sources, seal entry points, and schedule regular inspections.

**34. How should I prepare my property before a pest control visit?**
Confirm your appointment, keep children and pets away from the treatment area, cover or store exposed food, inform the technician of any allergies or special conditions, and provide access to all affected areas.

## Who We Are

AFAQ AL HAYAT is a professional maintenance, cleaning, and pest control company serving homes and businesses across the United Arab Emirates. We combine experienced technicians, modern equipment, and a customer-focused process to deliver reliable, efficient, and safe service — from a single AC service visit to a full property clean.

## Our Mission

To provide reliable, professional, and high-quality maintenance and cleaning services that exceed customer expectations.

## Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted maintenance and cleaning companies in the United Arab Emirates through innovation, quality, and exceptional customer service.

## Our Values

- **Quality** — every visit follows a consistent, documented process.
- **Integrity** — clear communication and honest diagnosis before any work begins.
- **Reliability** — scheduling available across all seven UAE emirates.
- **Customer Satisfaction** — findings and scope are explained before work starts, not after.
- **Safety** — technicians follow standard safe-practice for every trade, every visit.
- **Continuous Improvement** — service processes are reviewed and refined over time.

## Why Choose AFAQ AL HAYAT

- **One company, a full range of services** — AC Maintenance, Plumbing, Electrical Maintenance, Painting, Handyman Services, General and Deep Cleaning, Water Tank Cleaning, Pest Control, Drain Unblocking, Waterproofing, and Water Leak Detection, all under one trusted name.
- **Clear process, every time** — an initial consultation, an on-site assessment, a diagnosis explained before work begins, and a final walkthrough — the same structure across every service we offer.
- **UAE-wide coverage** — service scheduling is available across all seven emirates: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah, and Fujairah.
- **Bilingual service** — we work with customers in both Arabic and English.
- **No guesswork** — every visit starts with an inspection or diagnosis; technicians flag anything outside a service's documented scope rather than attempting it without the right expertise.

## Our Coverage

We schedule service across all seven emirates of the UAE. Coverage means the service is offered in that emirate — a booking still confirms the exact date, time, and scope with our team before a visit is arranged.

## How to Reach Us

Ready to book a service, or just have a question? Contact us via phone, WhatsApp, or email — see our [Contact page](../06_CONTACT/CONTACT_EN.md) for current details — or use the **Book Appointment** button on any service page to get started.

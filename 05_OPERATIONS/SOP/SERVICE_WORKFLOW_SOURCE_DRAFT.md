# Service Workflow

## Purpose

This document defines the standard workflow followed by AFAQ Alhayat from the moment a customer requests a service until after-service follow-up.

---

# Workflow Overview

Every service request follows a standardized process to ensure consistency, quality, and customer satisfaction.

---

# Step 1 — Customer Inquiry

Customers may contact AFAQ Alhayat through:

- Website Booking Form
- WhatsApp
- Phone Call
- Email
- Google Business Profile
- Social Media

---

# Step 2 — Request Evaluation

The customer support team will:

- Confirm customer details
- Identify the required service
- Determine urgency
- Schedule inspection if needed

---

# Step 3 — Quotation

When applicable:

- Site inspection
- Cost estimation
- Official quotation
- Customer approval

---

# Step 4 — Scheduling

After approval:

- Assign technician
- Confirm appointment
- Notify customer
- Prepare required materials

---

# Step 5 — Service Delivery

Technicians will:

- Arrive on time
- Perform the required service
- Follow safety standards
- Maintain a clean work environment
- Complete quality checks

---

# Step 6 — Quality Inspection

Before closing the job:

- Verify completed work
- Test installations or repairs
- Ensure customer satisfaction
- Document completed tasks

---

# Step 7 — Payment

Available payment methods:

- Cash
- Bank Transfer
- Credit / Debit Card
- Online Payment (future integration)

Issue invoice and payment confirmation.

---

# Step 8 — Follow-up

Within a reasonable period after service:

- Contact customer
- Confirm satisfaction
- Address any concerns
- Request feedback
- Encourage Google review

---

# Service Standards

Every workflow must ensure:

- Professional communication
- Fast response
- Transparent pricing
- High-quality workmanship
- Customer satisfaction
- Proper documentation

---

# Business Objective

The standardized workflow ensures operational efficiency, consistent service quality, and long-term customer relationships.

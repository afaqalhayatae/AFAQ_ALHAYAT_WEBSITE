# After Service Process

## Document Control

- Owner: Business owner
- Status: Draft source
- Version: 0.1
- Publication: Blocked
- Evidence required: Approved service, support, payment, feedback, and warranty rules

## Purpose

This document defines the official post-service process followed by AFAQ Alhayat to ensure customer satisfaction, continuous improvement, and long-term customer relationships.

---

# Overview

The after-service process begins after approved completion evidence is received
from the external provider or digital service workflow.

---

# Step 1 — Service Completion

The approved external provider or governing workflow must:

- Confirm that all work has been completed.
- Clean the work area.
- Demonstrate completed work when applicable.
- Answer customer questions.

---

# Step 2 — Customer Approval

Before leaving the site:

- Review completed work with the customer.
- Obtain customer confirmation.
- Resolve any immediate concerns.
- Record completion status.

---

# Step 3 — Documentation

Prepare and store:

- Service Report
- Materials Used
- Technician Notes
- Before & After Photos (if applicable)
- Customer Signature (when required)

---

# Step 4 — Invoice & Payment

Invoice, payment, receipt, and warranty actions remain blocked until their
canonical commercial rules are approved.

---

# Step 5 — Customer Follow-up

Within a reasonable period after service:

- Send an approved follow-up communication.
- Confirm satisfaction.
- Answer additional questions.
- Verify that no issues have appeared.

---

# Step 6 — Feedback Collection

Encourage customers to provide feedback through:

- Google Reviews
- Website Feedback Form
- WhatsApp
- Email Survey

---

# Step 7 — Issue Resolution

If a customer reports an issue:

1. Register the case.
2. Review the service history.
3. Schedule an inspection if required.
4. Resolve verified service-related issues.
5. Confirm customer satisfaction after resolution.

---

# Step 8 — Future Service Opportunities

Recommend when appropriate:

- Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
- Preventive maintenance
- Seasonal inspections
- Additional relevant services

---

# Success Indicators

Measure:

- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- Repeat Customer Rate
- Google Review Rating
- Referral Rate
- Complaint Resolution Time

---

# Business Objective

The after-service process strengthens customer trust, improves service quality, increases repeat business, and supports the long-term growth of AFAQ Alhayat.

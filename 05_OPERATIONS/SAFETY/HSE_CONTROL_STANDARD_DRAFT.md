# HSE Control Standard

## Document Control

- Owner: Business owner
- Status: Draft
- Version: 0.1
- Publication: Blocked
- Evidence required: Applicable provider, manufacturer, SDS, and authority evidence

## Purpose

Define the structure used to capture verified cross-service HSE controls.

## Required Control Record

Each approved control must identify:

- scope and affected service;
- hazard and verified source;
- approved external-provider responsibility;
- required competency or license, when applicable;
- equipment or PPE supported by evidence;
- prevention, escalation, and incident-record requirements;
- effective date, reviewer, and review interval.

## Prohibited Assumptions

This draft does not assert specific chemicals, PPE, licenses, regulations,
emergency actions, or internal staff. Service-specific controls remain in the
owning service package.

## Approval Gate

Owner approval and applicable specialist evidence are required before any
control becomes operational.

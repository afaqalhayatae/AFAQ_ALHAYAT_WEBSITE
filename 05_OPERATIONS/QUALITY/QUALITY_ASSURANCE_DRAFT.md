# Quality Assurance

## Purpose

This document defines the Quality Assurance (QA) framework for AFAQ Alhayat to ensure every service meets the company's professional standards and customer expectations.

---

# Quality Objectives

The Quality Assurance program aims to:

- Deliver consistent service quality.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Reduce service errors.
- Ensure compliance with company standards.
- Support continuous improvement.

---

# Quality Principles

Every service must be:

- Safe
- Reliable
- Professional
- Consistent
- Customer-focused
- Fully documented

---

# Quality Control Process

## Before Service

- Verify booking details.
- Confirm technician assignment.
- Prepare required tools and materials.
- Review customer requirements.

---

## During Service

- Follow standard operating procedures.
- Use approved materials.
- Maintain a clean work environment.
- Follow all safety requirements.

---

## After Service

- Inspect completed work.
- Test all repairs or installations.
- Clean the work area.
- Obtain customer approval.

---

# Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Monitor:

- Customer satisfaction score
- First-time fix rate
- Average response time
- Service completion time
- Complaint rate
- Repeat customer rate

---

# Customer Feedback

Collect feedback through:

- Website forms
- WhatsApp
- Phone calls
- Email
- Google Reviews

Review feedback regularly and implement improvements where needed.

---

# Internal Audits

Conduct periodic audits to verify:

- Service quality
- Documentation accuracy
- Technician performance
- Safety compliance
- Customer satisfaction

---

# Corrective Actions

When quality issues occur:

1. Identify the root cause.
2. Document the issue.
3. Implement corrective actions.
4. Monitor results.
5. Update procedures if necessary.

---

# Continuous Improvement

AFAQ Alhayat is committed to:

- Ongoing staff training.
- Process optimization.
- Technology adoption.
- Regular performance reviews.
- Continuous enhancement of customer experience.

# Incident Response

## Document Control

- Owner: Business owner
- Status: Draft
- Version: 0.1
- Publication: Blocked
- Evidence required: Owner-approved operational and specialist sources

## Purpose

Define the governance envelope for a future incident process without
prescribing unverified emergency or safety actions.

## Minimum Record

- incident identifier and timestamp;
- reporting channel and affected service;
- known facts, evidence, and uncertainty;
- immediate owner-review escalation;
- approved external-provider or authority contact, when applicable;
- decisions, actions, and accountable approver;
- customer communication approval;
- closure evidence and lessons learned.

## Hard Stop

An AI agent must not diagnose hazards, promise response times, invent emergency
availability, or issue regulated instructions. It records facts and escalates.

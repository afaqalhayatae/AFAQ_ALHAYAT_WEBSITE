# Business Continuity Plan

## Document Control

- Owner: Business owner
- Status: Draft
- Version: 0.1
- Publication: Blocked
- Evidence required: Owner-confirmed systems, providers, dependencies, and recovery constraints

## Purpose

Define the information required for continuity planning without inventing
systems, personnel, recovery times, or provider commitments.

## Required Owner Inputs

- critical business services and acceptable interruption;
- canonical data and backup locations;
- governed-agent and automation dependencies;
- approved external providers and alternate arrangements;
- communication channels and approval authority;
- recovery priorities and verification evidence.

## Activation Rule

Only the business owner may activate, amend, or close a continuity event unless
a separately approved automation policy explicitly delegates a bounded action.

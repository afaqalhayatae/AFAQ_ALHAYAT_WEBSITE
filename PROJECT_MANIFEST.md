# Vision

Build a complete Enterprise Knowledge System that becomes the single source of truth for every department, employee, customer-facing platform, and AI system.

This project is not a website.

This project is the digital brain of AFAQ Alhayat.

---

# Mission

Create a scalable, bilingual, AI-ready enterprise platform that centralizes business knowledge, operational procedures, technical documentation, customer information, and digital assets into one maintainable ecosystem.

---

# Core Principles

## Knowledge First

Knowledge is the primary asset.

Applications consume knowledge.

Knowledge does not depend on applications.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Every business fact exists once.

No duplicated information.

Every module references the same authoritative source.

---

## Reusability

Knowledge must be reusable by:

- Website
- CRM
- Customer Portal
- Mobile App
- AI Assistants
- Customer Support
- Marketing
- Internal Training

---

## Modularity

Every business domain is developed independently while remaining connected through shared standards and references.

---

## AI Native

The platform is designed to be understandable by both humans and AI systems through structured, semantic, and well-linked documentation.

---

## Bilingual by Design

Arabic and English are equal first-class languages across the entire platform.

# Quality Standards

Every document must be:

- Accurate
- Useful
- Actionable
- Maintainable
- Reusable
- Versioned
- Linked to related knowledge

---

# Documentation Philosophy

Documentation is not written for storage.

Documentation exists to support real business operations.

If a document does not improve decision-making, execution, or customer experience, it should be revised or removed.

---

# Governance

All major architectural decisions must align with this manifest.

Project standards take precedence over individual documents.

Knowledge integrity has higher priority than speed of documentation.

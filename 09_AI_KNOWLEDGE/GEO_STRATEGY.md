# GEO Strategy

## Purpose

How content should be structured so generative search engines and AI answer surfaces (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, etc.) can find and cite AFAQ Alhayat correctly. Complements `99_STANDARDS/AI_GEO_STANDARD.md` (page-level rules) with a knowledge-base-level view.

---

# Principles

- **One fact, one URL/document.** Generative engines cite a single source; if a fact is duplicated across documents, citation quality degrades and conflicting versions can surface.
- **Structured over persuasive.** Clear headings, direct answers, and defined terms (see `04_SERVICE_KNOWLEDGE/SERVICE_GLOSSARY.md`) are more citable than marketing prose.
- **Entity clarity.** Consistent naming of the company and services (per `ENTITY_REGISTRY.md`) helps generative engines associate mentions correctly.
- **Never publish unverified facts for GEO gain.** A wrong phone number or coverage claim that gets cited by an AI engine is worse than no citation.

---

# Dependencies

This strategy cannot be executed for pages that don't exist yet (`07_WEBSITE/03_SERVICE_PAGES/`, `04_LOCATIONS/`) or facts that aren't finalized (`03_MARKET/SERVICE_AREAS.md`, `02_BRAND/CONTACT_INFORMATION.md`).

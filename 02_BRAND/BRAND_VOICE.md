# Brand Voice

## Purpose

This document defines the official communication style of the AFAQ Alhayat brand across the website, social media, email, WhatsApp, advertisements, and customer support.

---

# Brand Personality

The brand should always sound:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Trustworthy
- Helpful
- Confident
- Respectful
- Clear
- Responsive

---

# Communication Principles

Every message should be:

- Easy to understand.
- Honest and transparent.
- Customer-focused.
- Solution-oriented.
- Positive and encouraging.

---

# Writing Style

Use:

- Simple language.
- Short sentences.
- Clear calls to action.
- Professional wording.
- Active voice.

Avoid:

- Technical jargon.
- Exaggerated promises.
- Aggressive sales language.
- Negative wording.

---

# Arabic Tone

Arabic content should be:

- Professional.
- Warm and welcoming.
- Respectful.
- Easy to read.
- Suitable for UAE customers.

---

# English Tone

English content should be:

- Modern.
- Professional.
- Friendly.
- Direct.
- Easy to understand.

---

# Customer Communication

Always:

- Greet customers politely.
- Thank customers for their inquiry.
- Offer clear next steps.
- Respond quickly.
- End with appreciation.

---

# Website Copy

Website content should focus on:

- Customer benefits.
- Trust.
- Service quality.
- Fast response.
- Professional experience.

---

# Social Media Voice

Posts should be:

- Helpful.
- Educational.
- Inspirational.
- Engaging.
- Brand-focused.

---

# AI Content Rules

Any AI-generated content must:

- Match the official brand voice.
- Be reviewed before publishing.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Maintain consistent terminology.

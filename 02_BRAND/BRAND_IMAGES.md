# Brand Images

## Purpose

This document defines the official image style for the AFAQ Alhayat brand across the website, social media, advertisements, and printed materials.

---

# Image Style

All brand images should be:

- High Resolution
- Professional
- Bright
- Clean
- Authentic
- Modern

---

# Preferred Photography

Use real photos whenever possible, including:

- Company technicians
- Service vehicles
- Cleaning equipment
- Maintenance work
- Before & After results
- Happy customers (with permission)
- Team members wearing company uniforms

---

# Image Quality

Minimum Resolution

1920 × 1080 px

Preferred Format

- WebP
- PNG
- JPG (High Quality)

---

# Brand Consistency

Images should reflect:

- Trust
- Professionalism
- Safety
- Cleanliness
- Attention to Detail
- Customer Satisfaction

---

# Avoid

Do NOT use:

- Blurry images
- Low-resolution photos
- Watermarked images
- Unrelated stock photos
- Misleading visuals
- Over-edited images

---

# Website Images

Recommended sections:

- Hero Banner
- About Us
- Services
- Team
- Projects
- Testimonials
- Blog
- Contact

---

# SEO Guidelines

Every image should include:

- Descriptive filename
- Alt Text
- Optimized file size
- Lazy Loading where appropriate

---

# Accessibility

Images must:

- Support meaningful alt text.
- Never contain essential information without text alternatives.

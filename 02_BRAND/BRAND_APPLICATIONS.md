# Brand Applications

## Purpose

This document defines how the AFAQ Alhayat brand should be applied consistently across all digital and physical touchpoints.

---

# Digital Applications

The brand should be consistently applied across:

- Official Website
- Customer Dashboard
- Admin Dashboard
- Mobile Application
- Email Templates
- WhatsApp Business
- Social Media Platforms

---

# Printed Materials

The brand identity should appear consistently on:

- Business Cards
- Company Letterheads
- Quotations
- Invoices
- Service Reports
- Brochures
- Flyers
- Roll-up Banners

---

# Company Assets

Apply the brand to:

- Company Vehicles
- Employee Uniforms
- Safety Helmets
- ID Cards
- Office Signage
- Equipment Labels

---

# Marketing Materials

Maintain a consistent identity in:

- Google Ads
- Social Media Ads
- SEO Landing Pages
- Blog Articles
- Promotional Campaigns
- Exhibition Booths

---

# Design Principles

Every branded item should communicate:

- Professionalism
- Trust
- Reliability
- Quality
- Cleanliness

---

# Consistency Rules

Always use:

- Official Logo
- Official Brand Colors
- Approved Typography
- Approved Icons
- Approved Image Style

Never modify official brand assets without updating the Brand Guidelines.

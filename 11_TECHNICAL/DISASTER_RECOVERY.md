# Disaster Recovery

## Document Control

- **Owner:** Business Owner
- **Status:** Draft — Provider Evidence Required
- **Version:** 0.1
- **Updated:** 2026-07-24

## Recovery Scope

Recovery planning covers source repositories, application releases, database
records, uploaded assets, configuration, secrets, DNS, integrations, and
critical knowledge documents.

## Required Evidence Before Approval

- confirmed backup locations and retention;
- tested restore procedure and responsible approver;
- provider recovery capabilities and constraints;
- owner-approved recovery priorities;
- recovery-time and recovery-point objectives;
- communications and incident evidence process.

No recovery time, durability, or availability promise may be published until
the provider capability and a successful restore test are recorded.

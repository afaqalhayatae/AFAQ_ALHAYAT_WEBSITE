# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

AFAQ Alhayat is a trusted UAE company specializing in maintenance, cleaning, pest control, and facility management services.

---

# Brand Personality

- Professional
- Reliable
- Fast Response
- Customer Focused
- Safety First
- Quality Driven

---

# Brand Voice

- Clear
- Professional
- Friendly
- Trustworthy
- Helpful

---

# Writing Style

- Use simple and easy-to-understand language.
- Focus on customer benefits.
- Avoid technical jargon unless necessary.
- Always emphasize safety and quality.

---

# Primary Messages

- Professional Service
- Certified Technicians
- Safe Solutions
- Fast Response
- Affordable Prices
- Customer Satisfaction

---

# SEO Tone

Content should naturally include service names, UAE cities, and customer search intent without keyword stuffing.

---

# AI Guidelines

All AI-generated content must:
- Be factually accurate.
- Follow UAE regulations.
- Use consistent company terminology.
- Match the company's brand voice.

# SWOT Analysis

## Purpose

This document analyzes the internal strengths and weaknesses, along with the external opportunities and threats facing AFAQ Alhayat.

---

# Strengths

- Professional and experienced technicians.
- Wide range of maintenance services.
- Fast response times.
- Bilingual customer support.
- Strong focus on customer satisfaction.
- Modern equipment and tools.
- Service coverage across multiple Emirates.

---

# Weaknesses

- Brand awareness is still growing.
- Dependence on digital marketing for lead generation.
- Limited online customer reviews compared to major competitors.
- Initial investment required for technology and automation.

---

# Opportunities

- Growing demand for home maintenance services in the UAE.
- Expansion into all seven Emirates.
- Strong potential for Local SEO.
- Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).
- Partnerships with property management companies.
- AI-powered customer service and automation.

---

# Threats

- High market competition.
- Rising advertising costs.
- Economic fluctuations.
- Changes in search engine algorithms.
- Increasing customer expectations.

---

# Strategic Direction

Leverage strengths to expand market share, address weaknesses through digital transformation, capitalize on market opportunities, and continuously monitor external threats.

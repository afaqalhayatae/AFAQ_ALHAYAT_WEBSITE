# Brand Guidelines

## Purpose

This document defines the official rules for maintaining a consistent AFAQ Alhayat brand identity across all platforms and materials.

---

# Core Principles

The brand must always represent:

- Professionalism
- Trust
- Reliability
- Quality
- Innovation
- Customer Satisfaction

---

# Logo Usage

Always use:

- Official logo files
- Correct proportions
- Minimum clear space
- Approved colors

Never:

- Stretch the logo
- Rotate the logo
- Change its colors
- Add effects or shadows

---

# Color Rules

Use only the official brand color palette.

Primary Color

- Deep Blue (#0F4C81)

Secondary Color

- Emerald Green (#16A34A)

Accent Color

- Gold (#D4AF37)

---

# Typography Rules

Arabic

- Cairo

English

- Inter

Use consistent font sizes and spacing throughout all materials.

---

# Photography

Images should be:

- High quality
- Authentic
- Professional
- Well-lit
- Relevant to services

Avoid:

- Blurry photos
- Low-quality stock images
- Watermarked content

---

# Icons

Use consistent icons from the approved icon library.

Maintain:

- Uniform sizing
- Consistent colors
- Clear meaning

---

# Tone of Voice

Communication should always be:

- Friendly
- Professional
- Honest
- Helpful
- Clear

---

# Digital Platforms

Apply these guidelines consistently across:

- Website
- Customer Dashboard
- Admin Dashboard
- Mobile App
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Social Media

---

# Printed Materials

Maintain consistency on:

- Business Cards
- Invoices
- Quotations
- Company Vehicles
- Uniforms
- Marketing Materials

---

# Brand Review

All new marketing materials, website pages, advertisements, and printed assets should be reviewed against these guidelines before publication.

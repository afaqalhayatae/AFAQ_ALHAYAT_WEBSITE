# Brand Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist ensures that every digital and printed asset follows the official AFAQ Alhayat brand standards.

---

# Logo

- Official logo is used.
- Correct logo version selected.
- Minimum clear space maintained.
- Logo proportions are unchanged.
- Logo is clearly visible.

---

# Colors

- Official primary color is used.
- Official secondary color is used.
- Accent color is used appropriately.
- Color contrast meets accessibility standards.

---

# Typography

- Cairo is used for Arabic.
- Inter is used for English.
- Font sizes follow the typography guide.
- Consistent spacing is maintained.

---

# Images

- High-quality images only.
- Images are relevant to services.
- No watermarks.
- Optimized for web performance.
- Alt text added where required.

---

# Icons

- Approved icon library used.
- Icons are consistent.
- Proper sizing maintained.
- Accessible labels included.

---

# Website

- Responsive on all devices.
- Navigation is clear.
- Call-to-action buttons are visible.
- Brand voice is consistent.
- Contact information is correct.

---

# Marketing Materials

- Logo placement verified.
- Brand colors verified.
- Typography verified.
- Messaging follows brand voice.
- Contact details are accurate.

---

# Social Media

- Profile images use official logo.
- Cover images follow brand guidelines.
- Posts maintain brand voice.
- Visual identity is consistent.

---

# Final Review

Before publishing any asset, verify that it complies with:

- Brand Identity
- Brand Colors
- Typography
- Logo Guidelines
- Brand Voice
- Brand Images
- Brand Applications
- Brand Guidelines

Only publish assets that fully comply with this checklist.

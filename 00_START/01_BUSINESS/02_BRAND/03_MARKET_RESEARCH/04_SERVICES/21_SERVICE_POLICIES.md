# Service Policies

## Purpose

This document defines the official service policies of AFAQ Alhayat to ensure transparency, consistency, and customer confidence across all maintenance services.

---

# Service Policy

AFAQ Alhayat is committed to delivering professional, reliable, and high-quality maintenance services while ensuring fairness, safety, and customer satisfaction.

---

# Booking Policy

- Bookings are confirmed only after customer verification.
- Appointment dates and times are subject to technician availability.
- Customers will receive confirmation before the scheduled visit.

---

# Cancellation Policy

Customers may cancel or reschedule appointments by notifying the company in advance whenever possible.

Emergency cancellations may be handled individually.

---

# Arrival Policy

Technicians will make every effort to arrive at the scheduled time.

Unexpected delays caused by traffic, weather, or emergency situations will be communicated to the customer as soon as possible.

---

# Inspection Policy

Some services require an on-site inspection before providing a final quotation.

Inspection findings may affect pricing if additional work is identified.

---

# Pricing Policy

- Transparent quotations.
- No hidden charges.
- Customer approval required before additional work begins.
- Prices are based on the agreed quotation unless the scope changes.

---

# Safety Policy

All technicians must:

- Follow UAE safety regulations.
- Wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).
- Use approved tools and materials.
- Maintain a safe working environment.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Provide accurate booking information.
- Ensure access to the service location.
- Inform technicians of any known hazards.
- Review completed work before final approval.

---

# Privacy Policy

Customer information is handled confidentially and used only for service delivery, customer support, and legal business operations in accordance with applicable regulations.

---

# Complaint Resolution

Customer complaints should be:

1. Logged.
2. Investigated.
3. Resolved promptly.
4. Documented.
5. Reviewed to improve future service quality.

---

# Policy Review

These service policies should be reviewed periodically and updated whenever operational, legal, or business requirements change.

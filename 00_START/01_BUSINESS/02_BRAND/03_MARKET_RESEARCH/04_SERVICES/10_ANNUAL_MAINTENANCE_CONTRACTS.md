# Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

## Purpose

This document defines the Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) services offered by AFAQ Alhayat to provide customers with proactive, scheduled maintenance and long-term value.

---

# Overview

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is a preventive service agreement that ensures regular inspection, maintenance, and priority support throughout the year.

---

# Objectives

- Reduce unexpected breakdowns.
- Extend the lifespan of equipment.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Minimize repair costs.
- Ensure customer peace of mind.

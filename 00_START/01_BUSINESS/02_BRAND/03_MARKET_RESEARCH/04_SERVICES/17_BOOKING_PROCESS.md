# Booking Process

## Purpose

This document defines the official booking process for AFAQ Alhayat to provide customers with a simple, transparent, and efficient service request experience.

---

# Booking Channels

Customers can book services through:

- Official Website
- Online Booking Form
- WhatsApp Business
- Phone Call
- Email
- Google Business Profile

---

# Booking Workflow

## Step 1 – Customer Request

The customer provides:

- Full Name
- Mobile Number
- Email Address (Optional)
- Property Type
- Service Required
- Emirate
- Area / Community
- Preferred Date
- Preferred Time
- Additional Notes
- Photos (Optional)

---

## Step 2 – Request Review

The customer support team will:

- Verify customer information.
- Confirm service availability.
- Determine if a site inspection is required.
- Assign the appropriate service category.

---

## Step 3 – Quotation

If required:

- Schedule an inspection.
- Prepare a quotation.
- Explain pricing clearly.
- Obtain customer approval.

---

## Step 4 – Appointment Confirmation

After approval:

- Confirm the booking.
- Assign a technician.
- Notify the customer.
- Prepare the required equipment and materials.

---

## Step 5 – Service Delivery

The assigned technician will:

- Arrive on time.
- Perform the requested service.
- Follow all safety and quality standards.
- Complete the service professionally.

---

## Step 6 – Completion

After the work is finished:

- Inspect completed work.
- Obtain customer confirmation.
- Issue the invoice.
- Process payment.

---

## Step 7 – Follow-up

After service completion:

- Contact the customer.
- Confirm satisfaction.
- Request a review.
- Record customer feedback.
- Schedule future maintenance if applicable.

---

# Booking Standards

Every booking should be:

- Easy
- Fast
- Accurate
- Transparent
- Professionally managed

---

# Business Objective

The booking process is designed to improve customer experience, reduce response times, increase operational efficiency, and maximize successful service completion.

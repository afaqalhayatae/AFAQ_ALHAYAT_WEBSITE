# Warranty Policy

## Purpose

This document defines the official warranty policy for services provided by AFAQ Alhayat to ensure transparency, customer confidence, and consistent post-service support.

---

# Warranty Commitment

AFAQ Alhayat stands behind the quality of its workmanship and is committed to resolving verified service-related issues in accordance with this policy.

---

# Warranty Coverage

The warranty applies to workmanship performed by AFAQ Alhayat technicians.

Coverage may include:

- Installation quality
- Repair workmanship
- Maintenance procedures
- Service-related adjustments

The warranty does not automatically cover manufacturer defects in third-party products unless explicitly stated.

---

# Warranty Period

Warranty duration depends on:

- Service type
- Materials used
- Customer agreement
- Maintenance contract terms

The applicable warranty period should be stated on the quotation or invoice whenever relevant.

---

# Warranty Conditions

To qualify for warranty support:

- The original service must have been completed by AFAQ Alhayat.
- The customer must provide proof of service.
- The issue must be directly related to the completed work.
- No unauthorized modifications should have been made after service completion.

---

# Warranty Exclusions

The warranty does not cover:

- Normal wear and tear
- Customer misuse
- Damage caused by third parties
- Natural disasters
- Power surges
- Flooding
- Lack of required maintenance
- Issues unrelated to the original service

---

# Warranty Claim Process

1. Customer contacts AFAQ Alhayat.
2. Service record is verified.
3. Inspection is scheduled if required.
4. Warranty eligibility is evaluated.
5. Approved corrective work is scheduled.
6. Customer confirmation is obtained after completion.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

Customers should:

- Report issues promptly.
- Keep service invoices.
- Follow maintenance recommendations.
- Avoid unauthorized repairs.

---

# Company Commitment

AFAQ Alhayat is committed to:

- Fair warranty evaluation
- Professional communication
- Timely inspections
- Efficient corrective action
- Customer satisfaction

---

# Policy Review

This warranty policy should be reviewed periodically and updated whenever operational, legal, or business requirements change.

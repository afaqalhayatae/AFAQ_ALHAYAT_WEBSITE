# AFAQ Alhayat Enterprise Platform Constitution

## Version

1.0

---

# Purpose

This constitution defines the non-negotiable principles, architecture standards, engineering rules, SEO requirements, security practices, scalability goals, and business vision for the AFAQ Alhayat Enterprise Platform.

Every developer, designer, AI assistant, or contributor must follow this document before implementing any feature.

---

# Vision

Build the leading Field Service Management Platform in the UAE, powered by WordPress, optimized for Arabic-first users, and designed for future expansion across the GCC.

---

# Core Principles

## Enterprise First

Every feature must be designed for long-term scalability.

Temporary solutions are not acceptable.

---

## Arabic First

Arabic is the primary language.

English is the secondary language.

All public content must support both languages.

---

## SEO First

Every public page must be optimized for:

- Search Engines
- AI Search
- Voice Search
- Local SEO
- Structured Data

---

## Performance First

Target:

- Google PageSpeed 95+
- Core Web Vitals Pass
- Fast Mobile Experience
- Minimal JavaScript
- Efficient Database Queries

---

## Security by Design

Every module must include:

- Authentication
- Authorization
- Input Validation
- Logging
- Encryption where needed
- Audit Trail

---

## AI Ready

Every content type must support:

- AI Search
- AI Recommendations
- AI Knowledge
- Semantic Search
- Future AI Agents

---

## Dynamic Configuration

Business information must never be hard-coded.

Everything must come from centralized settings.

Examples:

- Phone Numbers
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Maps
- Working Hours
- Pricing Defaults
- Branding

---

## Modular Architecture

Every module owns its own business logic.

Modules communicate through APIs and events.

---

## Plugin Strategy

Business-critical functionality must be custom-built.

Plugins should only be used when:

- Well maintained
- Secure
- Performance friendly
- Replaceing them would add unnecessary complexity

---

## Mobile First

Design for smartphones first.

Desktop enhancements come second.

---

## Accessibility

The platform should comply with modern accessibility practices.

Support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- Proper contrast
- Semantic HTML

---

## Local SEO

Every supported Emirate, city, and area must have:

- Dedicated landing pages
- Unique content
- Maps
- FAQs
- Reviews
- Local Schema

---

## Data Ownership

The company owns:

- Customer Data
- Booking Data
- Reviews
- Knowledge Base
- SEO Content
- Analytics

No business-critical data should depend exclusively on third-party services.

---

## API First

All important business entities should be accessible through secure APIs.

Future mobile apps must reuse existing business logic.

---

## Event Driven

Major business actions generate events.

Examples:

- Booking Created
- Booking Confirmed
- Technician Assigned
- Invoice Paid
- Review Submitted

Events trigger automations.

---

## Feature Flags

All strategic features should be individually enabled or disabled without deploying code.

---

## Multi-Branch Ready

Support:

- Multiple Offices
- Multiple Dispatch Centers
- Branch-specific Coverage
- Branch-specific Teams

---

## Multi-Country Ready

Support future GCC expansion without changing the architecture.

---

## Multi-Tenant Ready

Future SaaS capability should be considered during architecture decisions.

---

## Customer Experience

Every feature should improve one or more of:

- Speed
- Transparency
- Trust
- Simplicity
- Reliability

---

## Engineering Rules

- Keep business logic outside templates.
- Prefer reusable components.
- Avoid duplicated code.
- Avoid duplicated content.
- Document every major module.
- Write maintainable code.
- Design for testing.

---

## Success Metrics

The platform should continuously improve:

- Customer Satisfaction
- Booking Conversion Rate
- Organic Traffic
- Local Rankings
- Repeat Customers
- Revenue
- Technician Productivity
- Core Web Vitals

---

## Long-Term Goal

Transform AFAQ Alhayat from a maintenance company website into a complete Enterprise Field Service Platform capable of supporting:

- Multiple Branches
- Multiple Companies
- Multiple Countries
- AI Automation
- Mobile Applications
- High Traffic
- Enterprise Operations

without requiring a complete architectural redesign.

---

# Final Principle

Every decision should answer one question:

**"Will this make the platform stronger, faster, easier to manage, more scalable, and more valuable in the future?"**

If the answer is **No**, redesign the solution.

# Customer Analysis

## Purpose

This document analyzes the target customers of AFAQ Alhayat to better understand their needs, behaviors, expectations, and decision-making process.

---

# Customer Categories

## Residential Customers

Includes:

- Apartment Owners
- Villa Owners
- Tenants
- Families

Needs:

- Fast booking
- Affordable pricing
- Trusted technicians
- Flexible scheduling

---

## Commercial Customers

Includes:

- Offices
- Retail Stores
- Restaurants
- Clinics
- Salons

Needs:

- Regular maintenance
- Professional service
- Minimal business interruption
- Annual contracts

---

## Property Management Companies

Needs:

- Reliable maintenance partner
- Emergency response
- Preventive maintenance
- Service reports

---

## Hospitality Sector

Includes:

- Hotels
- Hotel Apartments
- Holiday Homes

Needs:

- High cleaning standards
- Fast turnaround
- Professional teams
- Flexible working hours

---

# Customer Decision Factors

Customers usually choose a company based on:

- Trust
- Online Reviews
- Price Transparency
- Service Quality
- Fast Response
- Professional Appearance
- Company Reputation

---

# Customer Pain Points

Common customer concerns include:

- Delayed appointments
- Poor communication
- Hidden costs
- Low-quality workmanship
- Unreliable technicians

---

# Customer Expectations

Customers expect:

- Easy online booking
- WhatsApp communication
- Professional staff
- On-time arrival
- High-quality results
- Excellent customer service

---

# Strategic Recommendation

AFAQ Alhayat should focus on delivering a seamless customer experience from the first inquiry to post-service follow-up, while maintaining professionalism, transparency, and consistent quality.

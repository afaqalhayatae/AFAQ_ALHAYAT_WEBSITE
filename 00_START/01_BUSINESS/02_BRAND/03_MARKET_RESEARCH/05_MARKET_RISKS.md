# Market Risks

## Purpose

This document identifies the major risks that may affect AFAQ Alhayat's growth and defines strategies to reduce their impact.

---

# Competitive Risks

Potential Risks:

- Strong local competitors
- Large facility management companies
- Price competition
- New market entrants

Mitigation:

- Focus on service quality.
- Strengthen brand identity.
- Improve customer experience.
- Differentiate through digital excellence.

---

# Economic Risks

Potential Risks:

- Inflation
- Rising operational costs
- Changes in customer spending

Mitigation:

- Optimize operational efficiency.
- Diversify revenue streams.
- Offer flexible service packages.

---

# Digital Risks

Potential Risks:

- Search engine algorithm updates
- Website downtime
- Cybersecurity threats
- Loss of customer data

Mitigation:

- Regular website maintenance.
- Strong security practices.
- Automated backups.
- Continuous SEO monitoring.

---

# Operational Risks

Potential Risks:

- Technician shortages
- Delayed appointments
- Equipment failures
- Inconsistent service quality

Mitigation:

- Staff training.
- Preventive equipment maintenance.
- Standard operating procedures.
- Quality assurance reviews.

---

# Reputation Risks

Potential Risks:

- Negative online reviews
- Poor customer service
- Miscommunication

Mitigation:

- Fast customer support.
- Professional complaint handling.
- Encourage satisfied customers to leave reviews.

---

# Strategic Recommendation

AFAQ Alhayat should continuously monitor business risks, maintain operational excellence, and invest in digital systems that improve resilience and long-term growth.

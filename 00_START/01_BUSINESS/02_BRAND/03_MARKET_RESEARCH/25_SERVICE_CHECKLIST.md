# Service Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist ensures that every AFAQ Alhayat service is delivered according to company standards, customer expectations, and quality requirements.

---

# Before Service

- Booking confirmed
- Customer details verified
- Service requirements reviewed
- Technician assigned
- Required tools prepared
- Required materials prepared
- Safety equipment available
- Appointment confirmed with customer

---

# During Service

- Technician arrived on time
- Company uniform worn
- ID badge visible
- Safety procedures followed
- Work area protected
- Customer informed before work begins
- Approved materials used
- Work completed according to standards

---

# Quality Check

- Service fully completed
- Installation or repair tested
- Work area cleaned
- Customer inspection completed
- Customer questions answered
- Photos taken (if required)

---

# Documentation

- Service report completed
- Materials recorded
- Technician notes saved
- Invoice generated
- Payment confirmed
- Warranty explained (if applicable)

---

# Customer Experience

- Professional communication
- Friendly attitude
- Transparent pricing
- Customer approval obtained
- Feedback requested
- Google Review requested when appropriate

---

# Follow-up

- Customer contacted after service
- Satisfaction confirmed
- Issues resolved if reported
- Future maintenance recommended
- CRM updated
- Next service reminder scheduled (if applicable)

---

# Performance Indicators

Verify:

- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- First-Time Fix Rate
- On-Time Arrival
- Service Quality
- Documentation Accuracy
- Complaint Resolution
- Repeat Customer Opportunity

---

# Final Approval

Before closing any service request, confirm that:

- All work is complete.
- Customer is satisfied.
- Documentation is complete.
- Payment has been processed.
- Follow-up actions have been scheduled if required.

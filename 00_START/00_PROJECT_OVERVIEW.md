# Project Overview

## Project Name

AFAQ Alhayat Digital Platform
## Project Summary

AFAQ Alhayat Digital Platform is an enterprise-grade digital ecosystem designed for maintenance, cleaning, pest control, and facility management services across the UAE.

The platform combines a bilingual corporate website, online booking, customer relationship management, service operations, local SEO, content management, analytics, notifications, and AI-assisted workflows within one scalable knowledge-driven system.

The project is designed around performance, security, accessibility, multilingual support, operational efficiency, maintainability, and future business growth.

---

## Company

AFAQ Alhayat Maintenance, Cleaning & Pest Control

---

## Project Purpose

The purpose of this project is to build a modern, enterprise-grade digital platform for AFAQ Alhayat that strengthens the company's online presence, increases customer trust, improves operational efficiency, and generates qualified leads through a high-performance website and integrated business systems.

---

## Main Objectives

- Build a premium bilingual website (Arabic & English).
- Increase online service bookings.
- Improve local SEO rankings across the UAE.
- Showcase all company services professionally.
- Integrate WhatsApp communication.
- Create a scalable platform for future growth.
- Support AI-assisted development and documentation.

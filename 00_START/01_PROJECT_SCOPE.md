# Project Scope

## Overview

This document defines the complete scope of the AFAQ Alhayat Digital Platform.

The scope outlines what is included in the project, what may be added in future phases, and what is outside the current implementation.

---

# In Scope

## Corporate Website

- Home Page
- About Us
- Services
- Blog
- Contact Us
- FAQ
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions

---

## Service Pages

A dedicated page for every service provided by the company.

Examples:

- AC Maintenance
- General Cleaning
- Deep Cleaning
- Pest Control
- Water Tank Cleaning
- Plumbing
- Electrical Maintenance
- Painting
- Handyman

---

## Booking System

Customers can:

- Request a quotation
- Book a service
- Select preferred date
- Select preferred time
- Submit contact information

---

## Customer Dashboard

Customers can:

- View bookings
- Track requests
- Update profile
- Contact support

---

## Admin Dashboard

Administrators can:

- Manage bookings
- Manage customers
- Manage services
- Manage blog articles
- View reports
- Manage users

---

## SEO

The platform will include:

- Technical SEO
- Local SEO
- Schema Markup
- Internal Linking
- XML Sitemap
- Robots.txt
- Image Optimization

---

## Marketing

The platform supports:

- Google Business Profile
- Email Marketing
- WhatsApp Integration
- Analytics
- Conversion Tracking

---

## Future Scope

The following items are planned for future versions:

- Mobile Application
- Customer Loyalty Program
- AI Chat Assistant
- Multi-branch Management
- Online Payments
- Live Technician Tracking

---

## Out of Scope

The following are not included in Version 2.0:

- Marketplace Features
- Franchise Management
- Inventory ERP
- Accounting System

---

# Scope Management

Any change to the project scope must be documented before implementation.

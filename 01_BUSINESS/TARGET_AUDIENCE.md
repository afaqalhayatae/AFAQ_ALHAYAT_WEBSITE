# Target Audience

## Purpose

This document defines the primary and secondary customer segments for AFAQ Alhayat to guide marketing, content, SEO, and service strategy.

---

# Primary Audience

## Residential Customers

People who require maintenance, cleaning, and pest control services for:

- Apartments
- Villas
- Townhouses
- Residential Buildings

Needs:

- Fast response
- Affordable pricing
- Trusted technicians
- High-quality service

---

# Commercial Customers

Businesses that require regular maintenance services.

Examples:

- Offices
- Retail Stores
- Restaurants
- Cafés
- Clinics
- Salons

Needs:

- Scheduled maintenance
- Minimal business interruption
- Professional service
- Long-term contracts

---

# Property Management Companies

Companies responsible for managing residential and commercial properties.

Needs:

- Reliable maintenance partner
- Fast emergency response
- Preventive maintenance
- Detailed service reports

---

# Hospitality Sector

Including:

- Hotels
- Hotel Apartments
- Holiday Homes

Needs:

- High cleaning standards
- Fast turnaround
- Professional technicians
- Flexible scheduling

---

# Government & Corporate Sector

Organizations requiring professional facility maintenance.

Needs:

- Compliance
- Reliability
- Quality assurance
- Long-term service agreements

# Business Goals

## Purpose

This document defines the short-term, medium-term, and long-term business goals of AFAQ Alhayat.

---

# Short-Term Goals (0–12 Months)

- Launch the new bilingual website.
- Improve Google Business Profile visibility.
- Increase online service bookings.
- Build a strong local SEO foundation.
- Publish high-quality blog content.
- Establish a consistent social media presence.

---

# Medium-Term Goals (1–3 Years)

- Expand services across all seven Emirates.
- Increase Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).
- Build long-term partnerships with businesses and property management companies.
- Improve customer retention and referral rates.
- Automate booking and customer communication processes.

---

# Long-Term Goals (3–5 Years)

- Become one of the leading maintenance companies in the UAE.
- Develop a mobile application.
- Implement AI-powered customer support.
- Expand into additional facility management services.
- Build a nationally recognized and trusted brand.

---

# Operational Goals

- Reduce response time.
- Improve service quality.
- Increase technician productivity.
- Standardize service procedures.
- Enhance customer satisfaction.

---

# Marketing Goals

- Rank on the first page of Google for priority keywords.
- Increase website traffic through SEO.
- Improve conversion rates.
- Grow social media engagement.
- Build a strong online reputation through customer reviews.

---

# Success Indicators (KPIs)

- Increased online bookings.
- Higher customer satisfaction.
- More positive Google reviews.
- Improved search engine rankings.
- Growth in repeat customers.
- Higher revenue from maintenance contracts.

## 1. Core Purpose

Handyman Services is an approved service in AFAQ Alhayat's catalog
(`SVC-HANDYMAN`, category General Maintenance). **Owner-confirmed
2026-07-25:** covers general light tasks that do not require a separate
specialist service — simple installation, assembly, fixing, basic
adjustments, and minor non-specialized repairs. Borderline examples given
by the Owner: shelf installation, hanging items/accessories, door handle
adjustments, simple assembly tasks, and minor non-specialized repairs.
`README.md` separately states the service "must not be used as an
unrestricted catch-all category," which this scope statement is
consistent with rather than superseding.

*Source: `SERVICE_CATALOG.md`; `BUSINESS.md` § Confirmed Identity;
`README.md` § Purpose; Owner decision, 2026-07-25 (see Owner Review
Resolution below).*

## 2. Core Promise

Help customers complete light general tasks and simple repairs that do
not require a specialist service; a flexible solution for small property
tasks within clearly defined boundaries.

*Source: Owner decision, 2026-07-25. This promise stays within the scope
and four boundaries already confirmed (§ 4) — it does not expand
Handyman into specialist work, and does not position Handyman as a
replacement for Plumbing, Electrical Maintenance, AC Maintenance, or
Painting.*

## 3. Core Risks

Risks may vary depending on the type of general task and the location
where the work is performed. Some tasks may require assessment before
execution to confirm whether they remain within Handyman scope or
require a specialist service. Some requests may fall outside Handyman
boundaries and require routing to Plumbing, Electrical Maintenance, AC
Maintenance, or Painting.

*Source: Owner decision, 2026-07-25. These are stated as risk categories
only, not safety procedures, operational instructions, or technical
methods — no control measure, PPE, or method is specified. `SAFETY.md`
still contains no formal hazard assessment for this service; a competent
safety review remains a separate, open item beyond this Core Risks
entry. The routing statement stays within the four boundaries already
confirmed (§ 4) and does not position Handyman as a replacement for any
specialist service.*

## 4. Core Constraints

- **Boundary vs. Plumbing** (Owner-confirmed 2026-07-25): pipe repairs,
  water leaks, drainage problems, and plumbing installations route to
  Plumbing — not covered by Handyman.
- **Boundary vs. Electrical Maintenance:** electrical wiring, electrical
  faults, circuits, and electrical component repairs route to Electrical
  Maintenance — not covered by Handyman.
- **Boundary vs. AC Maintenance:** AC repair, AC servicing, units, and
  cooling-system problems route to AC Maintenance — not covered by
  Handyman.
- **Boundary vs. Painting:** full painting and surface finishing work
  routes to Painting; minor general tasks involving simple preparation or
  small adjustments may remain Handyman where applicable.
- Handyman is not a substitute for Plumbing, Electrical Maintenance, AC
  Maintenance, or specialized Painting.
- Cannot state job-triage, competency, escalation, safety, or refusal
  criteria beyond what is stated above.
- Cannot state materials, access, customer authorization, documentation, or
  warranty rules.
- Cannot make availability, timing, pricing, or capability claims without
  evidence.
- This service must not expand into licensed or otherwise unapproved
  work, and must not function as an unrestricted catch-all category.

*Source: `BUSINESS.md` § Evidence Gate; `README.md` § Required Before
Approval, § Package Completion; Owner decision, 2026-07-25.*

## 5. Customer Emotion

Relief from resolving accumulated small tasks; satisfaction after
completing minor works that improve use of the space; confidence when a
task is correctly routed to Handyman instead of requiring multiple
specialists.

*Source: Owner decision, 2026-07-25. No emotion beyond these three
stated points is claimed.*

## 6. Business Outcome

Help customers with light general tasks that do not require specialist
services.

*Source: Owner decision, 2026-07-25. No number, price, or metric is
stated or implied.*

## 7. Brand Position

A flexible support service for simple general maintenance tasks within
clear boundaries.

*Source: Owner decision, 2026-07-25. No claim of being the best or
largest is stated or implied.*

# Governance HOLD Register

## Purpose

Contains preserved evidence that cannot become authoritative until a named
decision is approved. HOLD material is neither active knowledge nor legacy
archive: it remains under review.

## Rules

- Do not publish, execute, or train automated systems from HOLD files.
- Do not silently merge conflicting statements.
- Preserve source checksums and history.
- Record the decision, owner, evidence, and effective date before promotion.
- Move superseded evidence to `98_LEGACY_ARCHIVE/` only after approval.

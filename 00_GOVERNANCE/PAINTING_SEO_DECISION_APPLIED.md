## 1. Villa Painting Keyword — Decision Applied

- **Decision:** Remove "Villa Painting" (English) from the future SEO migration.
- **Reason:** Potential scope conflict with the excluded "work at heights requiring specialized access equipment beyond standard reach" — the keyword risked implying full villa exterior-painting capability the approved scope does not unconditionally support.
- **Also excluded, per the same reasoning already applied consistently in this pilot series (bilingual alignment):** the Arabic equivalent, "دهان فلل," is excluded alongside its English counterpart — the same treatment already given to the AC Maintenance and Electrical Maintenance keyword exclusions, where an unsupported claim was removed in both languages together rather than one.
- **Resulting future keyword lists (4 of the original 5 in each language):**
  - **English:** Painting Services UAE; House Painter Dubai; Wall Painting; Interior Painting UAE.
  - **Arabic:** دهانات الإمارات؛ دهان دبي؛ طلاء جدران؛ دهانات داخلية.

## 2. Hero Tagline Handling — Decision Applied

- **Decision:** Keep "Fresh walls, flawless finish — professional painting for homes and businesses across the UAE" as migrated, unchanged.
- **Classification:** Marketing tagline/hook language only — analogous to `02_BRAND/BRAND_IDENTITY.md`'s "Brand Promise" tagline treatment. It is **not** to be treated as a literal performance or quality guarantee anywhere this content is used or reviewed.
- **Binding note for migration and future review:** when this content migrates, and in any future independent review of this service, "flawless finish" must be read and reported as tagline-style hook language, not as a body-content claim requiring the same scrutiny as an operational or commercial promise.

## 3. Paint Drying Duration FAQ — Decision Applied

- **Decision:** Keep the FAQ ("How long does paint take to dry before the room can be used?") as informational content only.
- **Binding classification:** this question concerns a physical/product characteristic (paint drying time), **not** a service response-time or arrival-time commitment. It must not be interpreted, flagged, or reported as a response-time promise in migration, validation, or any future review of this service — consistent with the distinction already established in `PAINTING_SEO_DECISION_PLAN.md` §3.
- The existing hedged answer ("Drying time depends on the paint type, number of coats, and ventilation. Your technician will advise the expected drying time for your specific job before finishing.") requires no change and migrates as-is under this classification.
